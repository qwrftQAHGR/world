# 12. Combat and Action

## 1. Principles

- **No hit points.** Fights are measured in **injuries** (physical wounds), **kegare** (spiritual pollution, see 02), **bond-drain** (from Kodoku) and **Usure** (the MC's fading, see section 3).
- **Costs persist.** What a fight costs is carried out of it: kegare must be cleansed, wounds healed, fading repaired.
- **Recovery is the household.** The MC's Hearth (any sincere union) cleanses kegare completely. The MC's milk, kappa medicine, Inaba's cattail pollen and Tokoyo's tachibana heal wounds. Time with loved ones repairs bond-drain and fading.
- **Partners rarely die in battle.** Death in this setting comes mainly from the 49-day clock. In battle, partners are wounded, corrupted, bond-drained, captured or cut.
- **The MC is a fighter.** The MC fights on the front line with a spear and red thread, wielding partners as weapons.

## 2. Fighting in Utsushiyo

### The Ura Layer
- Spiritual combat happens in the **Ura**, overlapping the physical world (see 01).
- **Bystanders:** ordinary people do not see the fight. They walk through it, feel chills, and step around "nothing." A fight can happen in a crowded station at noon.
- **Spillover:** strong blows cause physical damage: shattered glass, dented cars, cracked concrete, power failures. **The Veil** rewrites spillover for witnesses: a gust of wind, a gas leak, a sinkhole.
- **Bystander danger:** bystanders can be hurt by spillover, by Magatsu that lunge at them, and by Kodoku isolation fields. Protecting bystanders is often part of a fight.
- **Kekkai:** a barrier field (four stakes, or a technique) pulls an area about 50 meters across fully into the Ura. Nothing inside spills out and no bystanders are inside. Kekkai take about 30 seconds to set up. The Harae-tai always uses them. The MC gains a version of their own at 36 knots (Hearth Barrier).

### Power in Utsushiyo
| Fighter | Power in Utsushiyo |
|---|---|
| Yokai (unaffiliated) | 50%; 100% at an anchor (a place where they are feared or believed in) |
| **The MC's knotted partners** | **75%** anywhere; **100%** within 100 meters of the MC |
| Humans (onmyōji, Harae-tai) | 100% (human magic is built for this atmosphere) |
| Magatsu and Kodoku | 100% (native) |
| The MC | 100% |

### Timing
- **Ōmagatoki** (twilight, about 30 minutes either side of sunset): Magatsu are at their strongest and most active. Crossings are easiest.
- **Night:** Magatsu are active; Kodoku hunt.
- **Day:** Tier 2–4 Magatsu are weakened by about a third in direct sunlight, but remain dangerous indoors and underground.
- **5–6 a.m.:** the lowest point of Magatsu activity in the city. Shūden is weakest at the first train.

### Jingikan Cleanup
After a visible incident, Jingikan cleanup crews arrive within hours to plant explanations and use Wasure-fuda on witnesses. The more often the MC's household fights in public, the more attention they draw from the Jingikan.

## 3. Kakurimi and Usure: The MC's Fading

### Kakurimi (隠身, Hidden Body)
When the MC channels En in combat in Utsushiyo, the MC **slips partly into the Ura**. Ordinary people's eyes slide off them, and they do not remember the MC clearly afterward. This protects the MC's identity in a fight. It has a cost.

### Usure (薄れ, Fading)
Each fight in Utsushiyo adds to the MC's **Usure**: how much the ordinary world is forgetting them.

| Usure | Effect on people who are **not** bonded to the MC |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | None |
| 3–4 | Acquaintances forget appointments with the MC; shop clerks don't notice the MC waiting |
| 5–6 | Coworkers, classmates and neighbors forget the MC's name and have to be reminded |
| 7–8 | The MC's employer loses their records; landlords forget the lease; old friends stop texting |
| 9 | Distant family must be reminded who the MC is |
| 10 | No one without a bond to the MC recognizes them. Records of them drift out of databases. |

- **Gain:** +1 Usure per significant fight in Utsushiyo. +2 for a fight using major techniques. +0 for fights in Kakuriyo.
- **Recovery:** –1 per week without fighting in Utsushiyo. A sincere, meaningful encounter with a specific person (a real conversation, a shared meal) restores that one person's memory fully.
- **Immunity:** **anyone with a thread to the MC** (red, white, gold, pink, blue) never forgets them. Partners, children, friends and loved ones are immune.
- **Effect:** the MC's ordinary human life holds together only through their relationships. A fighter who neglects their bonds becomes a ghost.

## 4. Fighting in Kakuriyo

- **Open:** everything is visible to everyone. Bystanders scatter, film it on their KD phones, or join in.
- **Legal:** combat in a city requires a license (see 02, 05). The MC is unlicensed at first. **Police response** in central Hyakki takes about **five minutes**: karasu-tengu fly in with wind fans, nets, ara-jū sidearms and shakujō staffs. Officers arrest unlicensed combatants regardless of who started it.
- **Collateral:** damage in Kakuriyo is real and visible. Owners sue. The Bunbuku Group broadcasts.
- **Terrain:** rooftops connected by silk lines, canals, lantern-lit alleys, airship moorings, foxfire plants, shrine forests, towers built for flyers with landing balconies.
- **Duels (kettō):** formal duels are legal in Kakuriyo when registered with the Kebiishi-chō, refereed and fought in a licensed ring or an agreed place. Tengu, oni and kitsune settle serious disputes this way. Duels are often broadcast. A challenge refused costs the refuser standing.
- **Power:** everyone fights at 100%. Human-cast magic is doubled (see 02).

## 5. The MC's Arsenal

### The Spear: Tsumu-boko (紡錘鉾)
- **Origin:** Tsumugi had two spindles. The great **Itomaki**, a meter tall, was taken by the Jingikan. Her **hand spindle (tsumu)**, a drop spindle of white mulberry wood about 30 cm long with a stone whorl, she hid beneath the floor of her main hall in 1909. She gives it to the MC.
- **Form:** at rest, a hand spindle that hangs at the MC's hip. In combat, it unfolds into a **spear** about 2 meters long: a white mulberry-wood shaft wound with red thread, a stone whorl as a counterweight at the butt, and a leaf-shaped blade of white stone.
- **Myth:** the spear is modeled on **Ame-no-nuboko**, the jeweled spear with which Izanagi and Izanami stirred the sea to create the first land. It is a weapon of creation.
- **Property: Unraveling.** A Magatsu or Kodoku struck by the Tsumu-boko does not just take a wound: its body **unravels**, coming apart into grey kegare-thread that streams off it. Heavy hits unravel Tier-3 Magatsu in a few strikes.
- **Property: Thread-lash.** The red thread on the shaft can be cast out to about 30 meters, with the stone whorl as a weight, for whipping, binding and swinging.
- **Unbreakable** by anything short of a god's weapon.

As the MC's body changes (bust growth, pregnancies), their fighting style changes with it. The blessing keeps the body fully capable. At high bust sizes, the MC fights from a planted stance and moves by thread.

- **Larger Magatsu and Kodoku are attracted** to the MC's life-force and come in greater numbers.
- The blessing keeps the MC mobile and strong for all seven days.
- The household's custom: partners guard a pregnant MC closely, and the MC usually refuses to stay behind.

## 6. Mitamashiro (御霊代): Partner Weapon Forms

### How It Works
In Shinto, a *mitamashiro* is the vessel a spirit inhabits. Each knotted partner gains a **weapon form**: they pour their soul into a weapon the MC can wield.

- **Consent:** the partner must choose to transform each time. **A Mitamashiro form cannot be forced**; trying would snap the thread. Some partners prefer to fight in their own bodies, and that is their right.
- **Calling:** the MC calls the partner by name (1 spool). The partner, if willing, dissolves into red light and re-forms in the MC's hand as their weapon. If the partner is far away, the MC can combine this with Thread-pull.
- **The weapon reflects the partner:** their species, powers and personality. (Seeded partners' forms are listed in chapter 14.)
- **Inside the weapon:** the partner remains fully conscious, can see and hear through the weapon, and **speaks in the MC's head**. They can advise, warn, complain and flirt.
- **Shared kegare:** kegare from the fight is split **50/50** between the MC and the partner-weapon.
- **Damage:** damage to the weapon is damage to the partner. If the weapon is broken, the partner is ejected in their own form, seriously injured (but not killed), and cannot take Mitamashiro form again until healed.
- **Leaving:** the partner can return to their own form at any time by choice.
- **Power:** a Mitamashiro weapon carries the partner's full powers, channeled through the MC's skill. A kitsune's foxfire bow fires foxfire arrows as strong as the kitsune's own foxfire.

### Awase (合わせ, Combinations)
When dual-wielding (from 18 knots), the MC can combine two Mitamashiro into an **awase technique**. The two partners must work together, and compatible partners (with strong sibling threads, or who are lovers of each other) produce stronger awase. Examples:
- **Foxfire bow + tengu war fan:** arrows of blue fire carried on a cutting gale, across a city block.
- **Water lance + ice tantō:** a lance that freezes on impact, encasing a Magatsu in ice.
- **Kanabō + silk gauntlets:** silk-bound club strikes that tether the target, so it can be swung into walls.
- **Nekomata claws + dream-net:** ghost-fire that eats Mushi swarms in an area.

## 7. Partners in Battle

- **Fighting alongside:** many partners fight in their own bodies alongside the MC, in true form. In Utsushiyo they fight at full strength within 100 meters of the MC.
- **Formations:** the household develops its own tactics: flyers above (tengu, harpies), shapeshifters and illusionists to misdirect (kitsune, tanuki), heavy hitters in front (oni, minotaurs), healers behind (kappa, baku, Nigi-dominant humans), and the MC in the center wielding whoever is needed.
- **Inaba** fights as a scout and skirmisher, using Kazoe-watari to cross crowds and rooftops, and kicks.
- **Un-gyō** fights as a defender, turning to stone to block.
- **Sibling threads** let partners sense each other within 100 meters and coordinate without speaking.

## 8. Enemy Doctrines

### Jingikan Harae-tai
- **Unit:** the kumi of four (barrier-caster, two gunners, seer-spotter).
- **Procedure:** spotter identifies the target. Barrier-caster plants four kekkai stakes (30 seconds). Gunners suppress with salt rounds. Suzu grenades stun. Ofuda tape seals wounds and binds.
- **Loadout:** carbines with salt rounds, ofuda tape, suzu grenades, kekkai stakes, Wasure-fuda, shikigami drones, peach-wood batons (for Yomi creatures).
- **Against the MC:** **Yui-tome cords** (結い止め), cords soaked in Shinkō from the Faith Grid, which bind a target and block thread arts while they hold. Standard orders are to capture, not kill.
- **Weakness:** dependent on the Faith Grid. Low morale. Many operators are Stained. Some can be persuaded.

### Onmyō Houses
- **Abe-ryū:** shikigami swarms (paper birds and paper beasts), Seiman star seals that burn spirits, divination to predict the MC's movements.
- **Ashiya-ryū:** curses at a distance: hair, a photograph or a footprint can be used to curse a target. The Dōman grid traps spirits.
- **Kurozumi-ryū:** drones, surveillance, seal apps, data.
- **Hanayagi-ryū:** kagura dance to call down a god's power, bells, dragon-blood water techniques.
- **Kuji:** all four Houses use the nine-syllable grid to block and bind spirits.

### Hasami-gumi and Kirigami-shū (the Bond-Cutters)
- **Targets threads, not bodies.** Their goal is to cut the MC's threads and let the clocks kill.
- **Weapons:** **enkiri shears** (long iron shears consecrated to Tachikiri), enkiri tantō, and the **Kirisaki-ito** (a wire garrote that cuts both necks and threads).
- **Tactics:** ambush lit, unknotted partners and cut their threads to drop the clock to 7 days; attack in crowds; retreat immediately after a cut.
- **Danzetsu** (断絶, Severance Field): a technique used by senior cutters that cuts **all threads** in a 20-meter area for about **ten seconds**. During that time, Thread-pull fails, Mitamashiro partners are forced back into their own forms, and sibling-thread coordination stops. Knots are not cut by it.
- **Weakness:** red silk cords spun by Tsumugi resist their blades. Jorōgumo silk armor turns their edges. Cutters are human and mortal.

### Magatsu and Kodoku
- **Magatsu:** territorial and predatory. They lure, ambush near their nests, and attack the weakest. Large ones smash.
- **Kodoku:** drawn to the MC from across a district. They **swarm**. Their **isolation field** (20 meters) blocks phones, muffles voices and, at full strength, **dampens sibling threads**, so partners lose each other's sense. Their bond-drain targets the MC's partners first, trying to make them forget the MC.
- **Counter to Kodoku:** saying a bond-drained person's name sincerely restores them. Touching them with a red, white or gold thread breaks the drain.

### Yomotsu-shikome (Hags of Yomi)
- **Speed:** move faster than the eye can follow.
- **Tactics:** hunt in packs of three or four; drag victims toward the Yomi border; herd Magatsu ahead of them.
- **Weakness:** **peaches** (thrown or as peach-wood weapons) burn them. **Food thrown on the ground** makes them stop and eat it, as in the myth. Bells.

### Kakuriyo Police (Kebiishi-chō)
- **Response:** karasu-tengu officers in pairs, then a squad in about five minutes.
- **Tools:** wind fans (knocking targets down), silk nets (bought from Jōren), ara-jū sidearms, shakujō staffs, and **license-suppression tags**: ofuda that seal a being's powers for an hour when slapped onto the skin.
- **Procedure:** they order fighters to stop, then subdue all parties and sort out responsibility at the station.

## 11. Example: How a Fight Plays Out

**Hachiōji, a weekday night. A Hōmu-gui has coiled under Platform 3 of Hachiōji Station.**

1. **Detection:** the MC, on the way home, sees grey sludge on the platform edge and an office worker swaying toward the tracks, with a Nemu-mushi on her eyelids and the Hōmu-gui's whisper in her ear.
2. **Entry:** the MC steps into the Ura (Kakurimi). The crowd's eyes slide off them. The MC flicks a needle into the worker's shadow, freezing her in place for a few seconds, and lashes the Nemu-mushi off her face.
3. **Engagement:** the Hōmu-gui surges up from under the platform edge, 20 meters of grey eel with a mouth of human teeth. The MC unfolds the Tsumu-boko and calls a partner: "Hayate." A karasu-tengu partner flows into the MC's hand as a black-feathered war fan (1 spool).
4. **The fight:** a gale from the fan slams the Hōmu-gui against the far wall of the track bed, and the window of a waiting train cracks; commuters blame the wind. The MC swings across the tracks on a thread anchored to the platform roof, drives the spear into the beast's side, and its body begins to unravel. It thrashes. A coil catches the MC (+7 kegare). The MC weaves a net across the tracks (1 spool) to hold it as a train approaches.
5. **Finish:** the MC strikes again, and the Hōmu-gui comes apart into grey thread that streams away before the train arrives. Hayate, in the MC's head: "Your footwork is a disgrace."
6. **Aftermath:** the worker blinks and steps back from the edge, unsure why she is crying. The MC has taken 7 kegare from the coil plus half of the gale's 6 (10 total), Hayate has the other 3, and the MC's Usure rises by 1. A Jingikan cleanup crew will arrive within the hour to explain the cracked train window. That night, the MC and Hayate go home to the Mayoiga, and the Hearth cleans them both.
