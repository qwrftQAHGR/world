# 05. Kakuriyo: The Hidden Japan

## 1. Overview

Kakuriyo is a parallel Japan of about **41 million** people. Its geography roughly matches Utsushiyo's: the same four main islands, coastlines, mountains and rivers, with some differences. Its divine sovereign is **Ōkuninushi**, who rules from Izumo. Its secular government is a parliament of great Houses and elected representatives in the capital, **Hyakki**.

It is fully modern. There are smartphones, bullet trains, skyscrapers, convenience stores, idol groups, office jobs, mortgages and income tax. The technology underneath is different: power from foxfire, an internet strung on spider silk, trains driven by spirit engines.

Kakuriyo is **dying slowly**. Since the Seal cut off human faith and fear in 1914, yokai births have fallen to 15% of their former level, natural rank promotions have nearly stopped, old bloodlines are disappearing, and the population is shrinking about 0.6% a year.

### Population

| Group | Share | Number | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kemonobito** (beastfolk) | 68% | 27.9 million | Working and middle class; rank Kotsu |
| **Yokai** | 21% | 8.6 million | Spiritually potent, politically dominant |
| **Ijin** (foreign monsters) | 7% | 2.9 million | Concentrated in Ura-Dejima and Shin-Dejima |
| **Kakushi** (humans) | 2.5% | 1 million | Descendants of the spirited-away |
| Other (kami, tsukumogami, unclassified) | 1.5% | 0.6 million | |

### The Sky and the Land
- The sun and seasons are the same as Utsushiyo's.
- **The moon** appears about 1.5 times larger than in Utsushiyo, and the shape of a rabbit pounding mochi is clearly visible on it.
- **The Milky Way** is visible as a bright band even over Hyakki's lights. On Tanabata (July 7), the stars Orihime and Hikoboshi visibly brighten.
- **The air** is thick with reiatsu. Colors are more saturated, sounds carry further, scents are stronger. Humans from Utsushiyo describe it as "everything turned up."
- **Wildlife** includes Magatsu, managed as pests and predators, and ordinary animals, some of which talk.
- **Forests** are older and larger. Much land that is farmland or suburb in Utsushiyo is still forest or mountain wilderness in Kakuriyo.

### Independent Realms Bordering Kakuriyo Japan
- **Kamuy Mosir** (Ezo; Hokkaido's equivalent): the realm of the Ainu kamuy (gods and spirits of the land, animals and elements). It never joined Kakuriyo Japan and is fully sovereign. A treaty from 1799 governs relations. Kakuriyo Japanese need permission to enter. Kamuy Mosir is neutral in Kakuriyo politics.
- **The Ryūkyū Kingdom of Kakuriyo** (Okinawa's equivalent): an independent kingdom of Ryūkyū spirits, including **kijimuna** (red-haired tree spirits) and **shīsā** (lion-dog guardians). It is a trading partner with an embassy in Hyakki.
- **Ryūgū**: the undersea domain of Watatsumi, sovereign over the seas of both worlds. The wani serve it.
- **Hōrai** (Penglai): the hidden realm of China, an island-empire of immortals (xian), Chinese dragons, huli jing (Chinese fox spirits, rivals of the Inari kitsune) and jiangshi. A major foreign power with an embassy in Hyakki and a large merchant community in Ura-Dejima.
- **The Westerly Hiddens**: what remains of Europe's hidden realms, a scattering of Faerie islands and enclaves much reduced since the Western Dimming. Most of their people emigrated to Kakuriyo as ijin.

## 3. Government

### The Divine Court (Izumo)
**Ōkuninushi** is sovereign over "hidden matters": spirits, yokai, bonds and fate. In practice he is a constitutional sovereign. He does not run the government, but:
- He presides over the **Kamiari Council** each autumn, where the gods decide the year's bonds (see 08).
- He confirms each new Sōdai (head of government).
- His **Hare Office** (Usagi-dokoro), staffed by white hares like Inaba, handles matchmaking cases and messages.
- His decisions on matters of fate are final and are not subject to the Yagyō-in.

### The Yagyō-in (夜行院, Parade Diet)
Kakuriyo's parliament, founded in 1917 after the Lantern Restoration. It meets in the **Gijidō**, a vast building of black timber and paper lanterns in central Hyakki.

**Upper chamber: the Ie-no-Ma (家の間, Hall of Houses).** 100 seats. Each of the **Hundred Houses** (Hyakke) holds one hereditary seat. A House is a great clan or institution. To qualify as a House, a clan must have at least **100 members of Yōkai rank or higher** and at least **one Kishin**. Houses that fall below these numbers lose their seat; the 100th seat is currently vacant because the **House of Kamaitachi** (weasel wind-sickle yokai) fell below 100 Yōkai in 2024. Several clans are competing for the vacant seat.

**Lower chamber: the Machi-no-Ma (町の間, Hall of Streets).** 300 seats, elected by citizens. Votes are **weighted by rank**:

| Rank | Vote weight |
|---|---|
| Kotsu | 1 |
| Yōkai | 2 |
| Ōyōkai | 5 |
| Kishin | 20 |

Before the 1952 Ōe Uprising the weighting was much steeper, and Kotsu could not vote at all. Weighted voting is the central political grievance of the beastfolk majority.

**The Sōdai (総代, Grand Representative).** Head of government, elected by the Machi-no-Ma and confirmed by Ōkuninushi. The current Sōdai is **Shinoda Rangetsu**, a six-tailed kitsune (Kishin) of the Lantern Party, cousin of the Inari Group's CEO. He is smooth, cautious and focused on the economy.

The governing coalition is the Lantern Party plus the Kemono Labor Party, an uneasy alliance of big business and labor.

## 4. Law and Enforcement

- **The Kebiishi-chō (検非違使庁)**: Kakuriyo's national police, named after the Heian-era imperial police. Dominated by tengu. Headquarters in Hyakki's government district. Hyakki has 24 precincts.
- **Sakai-bugyō (境奉行, Border Magistracy)**: guards the Kanmon checkpoint, patrols known thin spots, and hunts Mujina-michi smugglers. Part of the tengu military.
- **Power licensing**: Classes C, B, A and S (see 02). Unlicensed A-class use in a city: up to 5 years' imprisonment.
- **Humans**: a human from Utsushiyo who arrives illegally is detained by the Border Magistracy. Historically, such humans could not be returned and were settled among the Kakushi. The MC, who can come and go, is an unprecedented legal problem.
- **Marriage and the Enchō**: marriage is registered in the **Enchō (縁帳, Bond Register)**, run by the Inari Group under government contract. Kakuriyo marriage law allows **multiple spouses**, up to eight per person (the "Yae" limit, from the word for eightfold), with all spouses' consent. Long-lived species practice this commonly. The MC's 108 bonds exceed the Enchō's system by thirteen times and will require special legislation.
- **Courts**: civil and criminal courts in each province. Disputes between gods go to Izumo.
- **Prisons**: the main federal prison is **Ōmagadoki Prison**, in the mountains of Kai province, warded against every species' powers.

## 5. Economy

### Currency
- **Mon (文)**: the base unit. Copper and brass coins with square holes in the center, in denominations of 1, 5, 10, 50 and 100 mon.
- **Ryō (両)**: 1 ryō = 1,000 mon. Banknotes of 1, 5, 10 and 50 ryō, printed by the Inari Bank on fox-fiber paper that glows faintly under foxfire.
- **Approximate value:** 1 mon ≈ ¥10. A konbini onigiri costs 15 mon. A month's rent for a small Hyakki apartment is 80 ryō. An average monthly salary is 32 ryō.

### The Dōjima Shinkō Exchange
In Utsushiyo, the Dōjima Rice Exchange in Osaka was the world's first futures market. In Kakuriyo, its successor, the **Dōjima Shinkō Exchange** in Naniwa (the Osaka-equivalent), trades **Shinkō bonds**: certificates representing stored faith-energy held in shrine reserves. Shinkō bonds are bought by power companies, licensed exorcists, kami and investors. Since 1914 their price has risen steadily, because faith is scarce. The MC's activities will move this market: each restored shrine adds supply, and knowledge of the MC's existence will cause speculation.

### Goods from Utsushiyo
Goods from the human world (**utsushi-mono**) are smuggled through Mujina-michi tunnels and sold as luxury items. Most valued: Shōwa-era retro goods, human-made sweets and food (which carry faint traces of the cook's attention), manga, vinyl records, human-brewed sake, and anything handmade by a human. A bottle of ordinary Utsushiyo whisky sells for 40 ryō in Hyakki.

## 7. Daily Life

### Housing
Most people live in apartments. Standard features: Mushi-proof netting on windows, a small household shrine shelf (kamidana), species-adjusted bathrooms (deeper tubs for kappa, drains for slimes, perches for harpies), and a salt bowl by the door. Mixed-species buildings are normal.

### Food
- **Staples:** rice, fish, tofu, miso, pickles, mochi, sake.
- **Species favorites:** aburaage (fried tofu) for kitsune; cucumbers for kappa; fish and katsuobushi for nekomata; sake for oni and tengu; dango for tanuki.
- **Dejima-yōshoku:** Western-style food that came through Ura-Dejima, the Kakuriyo equivalent of Utsushiyo's yōshoku: omelet rice, croquettes, curry, plus foreign monster cuisine (lamia snake-wine, centaur oat breads, dwarven ale).
- **Konbini:** Inari Mart (blue sign with a fox), Pon Mart (orange sign with a tanuki).
- **Utsushiyo cuisine** restaurants serving "authentic human food" are a current trend in Hyakki.

### Education
- Compulsory schooling is nine years. Because species mature at different speeds, children are grouped by **developmental stage**, not age.
- Major universities: **Hyakki University** (the most prestigious), **Kurama Military Academy**, **Fushimi Commerce Academy** (Inari's business school), **Tōno Institute of Technology** (kappa engineering).

### Work
Office culture is less punishing than Utsushiyo's, but rank discrimination is pervasive. High-rank yokai dominate management, beastfolk fill most jobs, and promotion ceilings by rank are an open secret.

### Media and Entertainment
- **Television:** Bunbuku Broadcasting (BB), Pon-Pon TV.
- **Idols:** **KONKON48** (kitsune idol group, 48 members, the most popular act in Kakuriyo); **Nyandō** (nekomata dance unit); **Shirayuki Sisters** (yuki-onna ballad trio).
- **Kabuki** with real transformations; **rakugo** comedy, dominated by tanuki performers; puppet theater by tsukumogami puppets.
- **Sports:** **Kawa-zumō** (kappa sumo, the most popular sport; kappa love sumo in folklore); the **Kurama Cup** (tengu air races); kemari (traditional football) leagues; foxfire archery.
- **Streaming:** Kitsunebi Live streamers are celebrities. The top nekomata streamers have millions of followers.

### Religion
Everyone worships the kami. Ōkuninushi at Izumo is the highest authority. Inari shrines are the most numerous (every konbini has a small one). Kakuriyo's faith is weak: yokai and beastfolk generate little Shinkō compared to humans, and Kakuriyo's own kami are fewer and poorer than before the Seal.

## 8. Culture

### Gender and Sexuality
- Shapeshifting species (kitsune, tanuki, some others) treat sex and gender as flexible, and many change both over their lives. The term **utsuroi** ("shifting") describes someone whose gender changes.
- Femboys and femme non-binary people are common and ordinary, especially among kitsune and nekomata. Fashion styles that would be called "otokonoko" in Utsushiyo are mainstream.
- A neutral pronoun, **ware**, is widely used.
- **Futanari** (二形, "two forms") is an old Japanese word for people with both sexes. In Kakuriyo it is a known, rare natural trait among some yokai and a form some shapeshifters choose. The MC's body is unusual in Kakuriyo but not shocking.
- Same-sex marriage and multiple marriage are legal.
- Attitudes toward sex are frank. Fertility is revered because of the birth crisis. Pregnancy is celebrated openly; pregnant people get priority seats, gifts from strangers and blessings at shrines. Fertility shrines are among the most visited.
- **A full bust** is traditionally read as a sign of fortune and abundance, like a full rice bale. Ginkgo trees with breast-shaped burls (chichi-ichō) are prayed to for milk.

### Attitudes Toward Humans
- **Older yokai** remember humans: their fear, their prayers, their festivals. Many speak of the time before 1914 the way old people speak of their youth. Some hold grudges against human exorcists.
- **Younger yokai and beastfolk** know humans from smuggled manga, films and goods. "Human-chic" is a fashion trend: Shōwa-retro clothing, human-style cafés.
- **The Kakushi**, Kakuriyo's human minority, are treated as ordinary citizens but face mild prejudice: humans are considered fragile, short-lived and slightly comic.
- **Smell:** yokai can smell recent arrivals from Utsushiyo (*ningen-kusai*). Reactions range from nostalgia to disgust to strong attraction.
- **Human attention has value.** A human's sincere attention, prayer or fear carries Shinkō or Osore. A human visitor praying at a Kakuriyo shrine gives it more than a hundred yokai worshippers would. A human from Utsushiyo is valuable in Kakuriyo just by being there.

### Language
The same Japanese, with an archaic flavor. Old province names. Some Kakuriyo-specific terms:
- *Ningen*: human.
- *Utsushi-bito*: a person from Utsushiyo.
- *Utsushi-mono*: goods from Utsushiyo.
- *Kakuri-mono*: Kakuriyo natives (used by Utsushiyo spirits).
- *Kotsu*: low-rank; used as an insult when said with contempt.
- *Mieru*: to have sight (used of humans).
- "**Has your thread been cut?**" is a common expression for someone who seems lonely or lost.

## 9. The Birth Crisis

| Measure | 1914 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Yokai births per year | about 410,000 | about 61,000 |
| Natural rank promotions per year | about 12,000 | about 40 |
| Kemonobito fertility rate | 3.4 | 1.1 |
| Total population | 52 million | 41 million |

- Some old yokai species have fewer than 100 living members: **nue** (a chimera), **satori** (mind-readers) and **kamaitachi** (weasel wind-sickles; their House lost its Diet seat in 2024).
- Yokai who live long without faith or fear fade. They grow weak, translucent and forgetful, and eventually disappear. Some become Magatsu or Kodoku when they fall.
- Kakuriyo's government has run fertility campaigns since the 1960s, with little success.
- **The MC's fertility, blessings and promotions are the first real answer to this crisis in a century.** When Kakuriyo learns what the MC is, the MC becomes a matter of national survival.
