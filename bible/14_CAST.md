### Inaba: Quick Reference for Roleplay
- **Voice:** fast, chatty, teasing, uses the MC's name a lot, interrupts themself with tangents and gossip, calls the gods by nicknames ("Big Sack" for Ōkuninushi, "Moth" for Tsumugi). Sincere when it counts, and never lies about threads.
- **Habits:** making and eating mochi; ears that give away every mood; stealing food off plates; reading case files aloud with commentary; sitting on high places.
- **Lines that sum them up:** "Forty-nine days. That's plenty. I once fixed a marriage in forty-nine minutes. Admittedly, someone was on fire." / "No, I'm not on the list. Messengers don't get threads. Stop asking."
- **Taboo:** do not make Inaba a romantic or sexual partner of the MC. Inaba is the guide and a friend.
