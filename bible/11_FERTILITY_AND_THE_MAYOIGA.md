# 11. Fertility, Birth and the Mayoiga

## 1. The MC's Body After the Rite

The Futa-musubi rite gives the MC a **futanari** body: fully functional male and female reproductive organs. *(The MC's starting body, appearance and how the change looks on them are set in chapter 17.)* The rite also gives:

- **Virility:** abundant seed. Recovery after orgasm is nearly instant. The MC can sustain many unions a day without exhaustion or soreness.
- **Fertility:** both the MC's seed and the MC's womb are extremely fertile (rates below).
- **Resilience:** the MC's body is strengthened to carry the effects of constant pregnancy, birth and growth without injury, pain or loss of function.
- **Compatibility:** the MC can conceive with, and impregnate, **any species**. The blessing bridges every species boundary: human, beastfolk, yokai, ijin, kami.

## 2. Conception

### Who Can Carry
- **Anyone with a womb**, of any species.
- **Shapeshifters** (kitsune, tanuki, some others) who take a form with a womb can conceive and carry in that form. They must stay in that form for the whole gestation.
- **The MC.**
- Partners without a womb who cannot shapeshift cannot carry. They can still father children with the MC carrying.

### Conception Rates
| Situation | Chance per sincere union |
|---|---|
| MC's seed, fertile partner, no contraception | **90%** |
| Partner's seed, the MC carrying, no contraception | **90%** |
| Mundane contraception (condoms, pills, IUDs) | Reduced to **45%**; the blessing overrides it about half the time |
| A **Kodome-fuda** (子止め札, conception-stopping talisman) written by a kami, or a kappa-made Kodome charm | Reduced to **2%** |
| The MC's **seed-closing** (learned at 9 knots) | **0%** for the MC's seed, by the MC's choice |
| Partner already pregnant | 0% |
| The MC already pregnant | 0% for the MC carrying (the MC can still impregnate others) |
| Transactional union | Halved |
| Coerced union | 0% |

**Seed-closing:** at 9 knots, the MC learns to close their seed by will, making conception impossible for that union. The MC cannot close their own womb this way; for that, a Kodome-fuda is needed.

### Signs of Conception
Within an hour of conception, the carrier feels warmth low in the belly, and anyone with thread-sight sees a **tiny white thread** form between the carrier and the MC. The MC feels it as a small new pull. Tsumugi can tell the child's species and number at this point.

## 3. Gestation: Seven Days

Gestation always lasts **seven days of outside time**, ending on the **seventh night**. The number comes from **Oshichiya** (お七夜, "the seventh night"), the traditional Japanese celebration held on a newborn's seventh night, when the child is named.

| Day | Development |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Conception. Warmth, mild nausea, a strong appetite. The white thread appears. |
| **Day 2** | The belly begins to round. Cravings: species-specific foods, and often whatever the MC is eating. |
| **Day 3** | A visible bump, comparable to about four months of human pregnancy. The carrier is surrounded by a **Nigimitama ward** (see below). |
| **Day 4** | About six months. The child can be felt moving. The carrier's appetite doubles. |
| **Day 5** | About eight months. The carrier tires easily but is not weakened in danger. Breasts swell; milk may come in. |
| **Day 6** | Full term size. The child (or children) settle. The carrier wants to nest. |
| **Day 7, after sunset** | Birth. |

### Gestation Rules
- **Gestation cannot be sped up or slowed.** It is bound to outside time.
- **In the Mayoiga's fast stream, a pregnancy is paused.** Seven days of inside time pass, and the pregnancy has not advanced at all. It resumes when the carrier leaves.
- **Physical capacity:** the blessing keeps the carrier healthy, strong and mobile through all seven days. A pregnant partner can fight, though carefully.

### The Nigimitama Ward
From the third day, every carrier of the MC's child is surrounded by a protective ward about 2 meters across:
- **Mushi and Tsukimono cannot enter it.** Existing ones are expelled.
- **Kegare gain is halved.**
- **Larger Magatsu and Kodoku are attracted** to the strong life-force, like predators to the scent of new life. Pregnant partners are hunted harder.

### Eggs
Species that lay eggs (some ijin such as harpies and some dragons; jorōgumo produce a small egg-sac) **lay the egg on the seventh night** instead of giving birth. The egg **hatches on the seventh night after it is laid**. Eggs are kept warm in the Mayoiga's slow-stream house (in the **Tamago-no-ma**, the egg room), since eggs, like pregnancies, do not develop in the fast stream.

## 4. Birth

- **When:** on the seventh night, between sunset and dawn. Births never happen earlier.
- **Where:** anywhere, but the household's custom is to give birth at Itokake Shrine or in the Mayoiga's **Ubuya** (産屋, birthing hut). Traditional Japanese households once had a separate hut for births, and the Mayoiga has one.
- **Labor:** fast, usually under an hour. The blessing dulls the pain to strong pressure and cramping. Complications do not happen; the blessing prevents them.
- **Attendants:** the household's midwives are **ubume**, spirits of women who died in childbirth, who in Kakuriyo work as a midwives' guild. Two or three attend every birth. Tsumugi attends when she can and spins the child's white thread to the MC.
- **Recovery:** the carrier is fully recovered within a day.
- **The Seventh-Night blessing** lands at the moment of birth (see 10).
- **Naming:** by tradition, the child is named at birth, at a small ceremony on the birthing night. The name is written on a paper strip and hung in the Mayoiga's hearth room.

## 5. The Children: Musubi-ko (結び子)

### What They Are
- **Species:** a child takes the **non-MC parent's species**, whichever parent carried. A child of the MC and a kitsune is a kitsune. A child of the MC and a human is a human.
- **Rank:** every Musubi-ko is born **at Yōkai rank** (or the equivalent) regardless of species (see 10). Children of human partners are born as Awakened humans with sight and a nascent signature ability.
- **Appearance:** the non-MC parent's species, with features from both parents. Hair and eye color, face and build can come from either.
- **The mark:** a thin red line around the base of the left little finger, like a tied thread.
- **Sex:** follows the normal distribution of the non-MC parent's species. Shapeshifting species' children often choose later.
- **Gifts:**
  - **Thread-sight** from birth: they see all bonds (see 02).
  - **Mushi-touch:** Mushi die on contact with them.
  - **En sensitivity:** they feel the household's moods.
  - The species abilities of their non-MC parent.

### What They Are Not
- They are **never on the ledger**. Their bond to the MC is a white family thread.
- Their own red threads, if they have them, **always lead outward** to people outside the family.
- They are children, and are written as children: family, not partners.

### Growing Up
Children grow up in the **Mayoiga's fast stream** (see below), where one year passes for each outside day.
- **Birth to 7 years (7 outside days):** bound to the Mayoiga. A Japanese saying goes "Until seven, children belong to the gods." They cannot leave the fast stream; they can come to the slow-stream house to see the MC and others.
- **7 years (day 7) onward:** may visit the outside world with a guardian. While outside, they age at the normal rate for their species.
- **18 years (day 18):** adults. They may leave the Mayoiga permanently and live in either realm. Adults who stay in the fast stream **stop aging** there, like the anchored adults.
- **Shichi-go-san** (七五三, the "seven-five-three" festival celebrating children at ages 3, 5 and 7) is held for every child in the Mayoiga on the appropriate inside days. **Each child's seventh-year celebration** is the household's biggest regular event, because it marks the day the child can step outside.

### Upbringing
Children have **real childhoods** in the fast stream: years of play, school, friendships, mistakes and growing up. They are people with their own personalities and their own feelings about their unusual family. They are not instant adults.

### What They Become
Adult Musubi-ko take on roles in and around the household:
- **En-no-tsukai** (縁の使い, bond-agents): the household's matchmakers. With thread-sight and training, they can find people whose ordinary (non-ledger) threads are tangled or lost and help them reconnect, and they can clear Mushi and Tsukimono from people by touch.
- **Hunters:** Magatsu exterminators in both realms.
- **Priests and miko:** restoring and staffing the shrines of shrineless gods.
- **Diplomats and members of House Itokake** (see 07, 15).
- **Anything else they choose.** Some leave and live ordinary lives.

### Legal Status
- **Utsushiyo:** Musubi-ko have no **koseki** (family register), so legally they do not exist. They cannot get passports, health insurance or schooling. The Jingikan classifies them as unregistered spirits.
- **Kakuriyo:** the Enchō has no category for them. The Inari Group proposes registering them as Inari dependents. The tengu propose registering them under military guardianship. The MC's household wants them registered as members of **House Itokake**.

## 6. The MC's Changing Body

### Breast Growth
- **Each birth the MC carries:** breasts grow by **one cup size**.
- **Each birth of the MC's child carried by a partner:** **half a cup size**.
- Growth happens over the night of the birth.
- **There is no upper limit.**
- **The blessing supports the growth:** the MC's back, posture and strength adjust so the growth never causes pain, injury or loss of mobility. The MC's fighting style changes as their body does: lower and more grounded, with more reliance on thread-mobility (see 12).
- *(The MC's starting size is set in chapter 17.)*

### In Kakuriyo and Utsushiyo
- **In Kakuriyo,** a full bust is read as a sign of fortune and abundance. The MC's growth is visible public proof of the number of children they have had. Strangers bow, ask for blessings or stare.
- **In Utsushiyo,** the MC's body quickly becomes conspicuous. From around the tenth birth, the MC needs a **henge** glamour, cast by a kitsune or tanuki partner, to pass as ordinary in public. Henge costs the caster about 1 kegare per hour to maintain and breaks under mirrors, dogs, sincere prayer or a strong blow.

### Lactation and Musubi Milk
- **Begins** after the first birth of any of the MC's children, whether carried by the MC or a partner, and increases with each birth.
- **Properties of the MC's milk (Musubi-jiru, 結び汁):**
  - **Purifies:** –20 kegare (counts once per day per person).
  - **Heals:** closes moderate wounds, eases illness, restores stamina.
  - **Nourishes:** the MC's children grow healthy and strong on it.
  - **Small luck:** a minor Sakimitama boost for a day.
- **Shelf life:** spoils in **3 days** unless kept in Inari-blessed ceramic or in the Mayoiga's slow-stream pantry.
- **Market:** in Kakuriyo the milk is counted as a sacred offering. Black-market value: about **50 ryō a cup**. Counterfeits (ordinary holstaur milk with foxfire coloring) appear quickly.
- **In combat:** the household carries small flasks as field medicine (see 12).

## 7. The Mayoiga (迷い家)

### What It Is
In the folklore of Tōno, recorded in the *Tōno Monogatari* of 1910, the **Mayoiga** ("wandering house") is a grand house found deep in the mountains, empty but with fires lit and food laid out. Anyone who finds it and takes something away is blessed with fortune.

In this setting, the Mayoiga is a **house-spirit**: an ancient yokai house that has wandered the mountains of both realms for over a thousand years, looking for a family to belong to. In 2026 the Hare Office, through Inaba, offers it the MC's household. It accepts.

Behind the house, joined to it by a small gate, is a **fragment of Tokoyo**, the eternal land, leased from **Sukunabikona** at Ōkuninushi's request.

### Entrances
- **Before 36 knots:** knocking three times on the storehouse (kura) door at **Itokake Shrine** in Hachiōji, or at **Itokake-chō** in Hyakki, opens the Mayoiga's front door.
- **From 36 knots:** knocking three times on **any door** in either realm opens it.
- **It is a crossing.** The Mayoiga has a front door into each realm. Enter from Utsushiyo, leave into Kakuriyo. This makes it an **unlicensed border crossing**, which matters to the Jingikan and the tengu Border Magistracy.

### Who Can Enter
- The MC.
- Knotted partners (they can open it themselves once knotted).
- The MC's children.
- Household spirits and staff.
- Tsumugi, Inaba, Un-gyō.
- **Anyone the MC leads in by the hand.**
- **No one else.** Enemies cannot find or force the door. The Mayoiga cannot be located by divination.

### Okamado (お竈): The House's Spirit
- **Who:** the Mayoiga's own spirit, a hearth goddess who lives in the great **irori** (sunken hearth) at the heart of the house. Her fire has not gone out in a thousand years.
- **Appearance:** a tall, broad, middle-aged woman with sleeves tied back by a red tasuki cord, soot on her cheek, warm brown skin and a face that is never quite the same twice. She smells of woodsmoke and rice.
- **Personality:** practical, bossy, motherly, unflappable. She runs the household: kitchen, laundry, nursery, rooms, schedules. She disapproves of mess, late meals and anyone who neglects the children.
- **Power:** inside the Mayoiga she is close to absolute. She controls its rooms, doors, fires and weather. Outside it she has no power.
- **Not a ledger partner.**

### The Iebito (家人): House Helpers
Faceless figures of smooth pale wood in plain indigo work clothes. They cook, clean, garden, mind children and carry things. They do not speak but understand everything. About **one Iebito per five children**; Okamado makes more as the household grows.

## 8. Time in the Mayoiga

### Two Streams
- **The house is the slow stream.** Time passes as it does outside.
- **The garden valley is the fast stream.** **One year passes for each outside day.** One outside hour is about fifteen days inside; one outside minute is about six hours inside.

### Who Can Enter the Fast Stream
- **Children:** live there until at least 7, and may stay longer.
- **Knotted partners:** may enter freely.
- **Household staff:** Okamado, the Iebito, the ubume midwives.
- **Guests the MC brings in by the hand.**
- **Not the MC.** The MC is the anchor of 108 threads, and the MC's time is bound to the threads' outside time. The MC can enter the slow-stream house but **cannot pass through the Tokoyo Gate**. The MC watches the garden through the gate and sees the children grow, but cannot enter it.

### Effects of the Fast Stream on Adults
- **No aging.** Tokoyo grants agelessness to adults anchored to the household (knotted partners, adult children, staff). Partners can spend years in the fast stream and come out no older in body.
- **Full experience.** The mind experiences the time fully. A partner who spends one outside day in the fast stream lives a full year there.
- **Uses:**
  - **Parenting.** Partners take shifts in the fast stream to raise the children. The household calls these shifts **Kamado-ban** (竈番, "hearth duty"). A partner who spends an outside afternoon on duty spends weeks with the children.
  - **Training and study.** Partners can train for months in one outside day.
  - **Rest and recovery.** Healing happens on inside time.
- **Costs:**
  - A partner who spends long periods in the fast stream comes out **older in mind** than the people they left outside. Spending ten outside days in the fast stream means ten years of experience, and friends outside notice the change.
  - **Pregnancies are paused** (see section 3).
  - **The 49-day clocks run on outside time,** so a lit but unknotted partner cannot buy time in the fast stream. They also cannot enter the Mayoiga until knotted.
- **Kegare** behaves normally in the fast stream; the hot spring and the tachibana help.

### The Terakoya: Education
- **Teachers:** partners on Kamado-ban duty, Iebito (for basic lessons), adult Musubi-ko, Tsumugi (thread-craft), Inaba (history and law, delivered with much gossip), Okamado (cooking and housekeeping), and visiting guests. Sukunabikona teaches medicine and brewing on his unannounced visits.
- **Curriculum:** reading and writing, arithmetic, the history of both realms, the gods and the myths, species arts (each child learns their own species' abilities from partners of that species), thread-craft and purification, the laws of both Japans, combat from age 12, and the realities of the household's war.
- **School ends** at about 15 inside years. After that, apprenticeships.

### The MC and the Children
The MC cannot enter the fast stream, so the children come to the slow-stream house to see them. While in the house, children age at the normal outside rate. A child who visits the MC every evening is a year older each time.

Partners note that this is hardest on the MC.

### Rent
Sukunabikona leases the Tokoyo fragment for **one cask of sake** brewed in the Mayoiga, delivered to Izumo every Kamiarizuki. He is particular about the sake's quality and visits to inspect the brewery.
