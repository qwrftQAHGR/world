# 15. Calendar, Status Quo and Plot

## 1. The Annual Calendar

Festivals have concrete effects in this setting.

| Date | Festival | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Jan 1–3** | **Shōgatsu** (New Year), **Hatsumōde** (first shrine visit) | Tens of millions of people visit shrines in Utsushiyo. Every active shrine receives a large surge of Shinkō. A restored shrine (Itokake, any restored shrineless god's) gains enough faith in three days for a year. Konohanasakuya-hime appears in public on Mount Fuji in Kakuriyo. |
| **Feb 3** | **Setsubun** (bean-throwing, the eve of spring) | People throw roasted soybeans while shouting "Oni out, fortune in!" In Utsushiyo, scattered beans drive out Mushi, and Magatsu activity falls for a week. In Kakuriyo, **the oni unions strike** in protest every year, and Ōe-gashi shuts down. Oni partners have a bad day. The seasonal boundary makes crossings slightly easier. |
| **Mar 3** | **Hinamatsuri** (Dolls' Festival) | **Nagashi-bina**: paper dolls are floated down rivers to carry away impurity. Each floated doll removes 10 kegare from the person who made it. The Mayoiga holds a festival for its daughters. |
| **Late March** | **Spring Higan** (equinox week) | The dead are closer. Ancestral spirits can be spoken to at graves. |
| **Early April** | **Hanami** (cherry-blossom viewing) | Konohanasakuya's season. Mushi die off in happy crowds under the blossoms. Magatsu avoid parks. |
| **May 5** | **Kodomo no Hi** (Children's Day) | Carp streamers. A festival for all the Mayoiga's children. |
| **June 30** | **Nagoshi no Ōharae** (Summer Great Purification) | Shrines set up a **chinowa**, a large ring of grass that people walk through to be cleansed (–40 kegare). The Jingikan performs a city-wide purification. **Tokyo's Magatsu numbers fall by about 30% for a month.** |
| **July 7** | **Tanabata** (Star Festival) | Orihime and Hikoboshi meet across the Milky Way. **The Shimenawa weakens for one night.** Crossings at old shrines open for anyone with strong intent. Threads glow, visible to anyone with any sight. **Ledger threads light at 100 meters** instead of 10. Orihime can be contacted. The Jingikan guards every major shrine. |
| **Aug 13–16** | **Obon** (Festival of the Dead) | The dead return to visit their families. **Yomi's traffic peaks:** Magatsu activity doubles, hags are active, and stuck ghosts are restless. Ancestral spirits are strongest. Bon dances repel Mushi. On the last night, **Okuribi** (sending-off fires) guide the dead back. |
| **Aug 16** (last night of Obon) | **Hyakki Yagyō Festival** (Kakuriyo) | The great Night Parade through Hyakki: tens of thousands of yokai, beastfolk and ijin in procession along the lantern route through six wards, with floats, music and foxfire. Kakuriyo's biggest festival. |
| **Mid-September** | **Tsukimi** (Moon-viewing) | The moon rabbits' festival. Inaba's favorite holiday: an enormous amount of mochi. In Kakuriyo, the rabbit on the moon is clearly visible. |
| **10th lunar month** (≈ late Oct–Nov) | **Kannazuki / Kamiarizuki** | The gods leave their shrines for Izumo. **In Utsushiyo, the shrines are empty for a month:** the Faith Grid weakens, licensed exorcists lose about half their power, and **Magatsu activity rises sharply**. This is the Jingikan's hardest month. **In Izumo:** the **Kamiari Council** (see 08). In 2026, gods arrive at Inasa Beach on **November 19** and stay seven days. |
| **Nov 15** | **Shichi-go-san** | Celebrations for children aged 3, 5 and 7. The Mayoiga holds its largest children's festival. |
| **Early November** | **Itokake Festival** (Kakuriyo) | Itokake-chō's shrine festival, held every year for 117 years without its goddess. A silk procession, looms in the streets, red cords given away. |
| **Dec 31** | **Ōmisoka** (New Year's Eve), **Joya no Kane**, winter **Ōharae** | Temple bells ring **108 times** to cleanse the 108 earthly desires. **The Jingikan reinforces the Seal** on the 108th toll in a rite beneath Sōmusubi Jingū. The MC feels every bell ring through their 108 hooks, and each tied knot rings back. Winter Ōharae: –40 kegare to attendees. |

## 2. Status Quo at the Start

The story begins on **Day 1**: the day the MC prays at Itokake Shrine. *(The exact date and the MC's circumstances are set in chapter 17. Default: early April 2026, during cherry-blossom season, so that the first year runs through Tanabata, Obon, the Kamiari Council and Ōmisoka.)*

### On Day 1
| Element | State |
|---|---|
| Knots | 0 |
| Lit threads | 0 |
| Tsumugi | Barely awake, translucent, unable to leave the shrine |
| Itokake Shrine | Overgrown, cracked, dry |
| Inaba | Arrives on Day 1 or Day 2 with a case file and a bag of mochi |
| The Mayoiga | Arrives within the first week, after Inaba arranges it |
| The Jingikan | Seal instruments in the Sōmusubi vault register Itokake waking within 24 hours. **Kirishima Aoi** is assigned to observe by Day 4. |
| The Kirigami-shū | Learns of the MC through a leak from the Hasami-gumi in about **three weeks** |
| Kakuriyo | Itokake-chō feels Tsumugi wake (every loom stops). Kakuriyo at large learns of the MC **on their first public appearance in Hyakki**, usually through Tamaki's stream. |
| Yomi | Kuro-ikazuchi and the hags notice the MC after the **first birth** |
| The Unclaimed | Stirs at Kōyōdai Danchi on Day 1. Its Kodoku start drifting toward Itokake within days. |

### Faction Clocks
Background threats that advance over time unless the MC's household acts.

| Clock | Stages |
|---|---|
| **Jingikan** | Observe (weeks 1–4) → Contain: capture orders, Hasami-gumi deployed (from the first visible knot) → **Ito-bashira**: Mibu moves to make the MC the Seal's pillar (by Ōmisoka of the first year, if not stopped) |
| **Kirigami-shū** | Learn (week 3) → Cut: target lit threads (week 4 on) → Strike: a major attack on the household, likely on Tanabata → Danzetsu on the Mayoiga's doors |
| **Yomi's crack** | Baseline: Yomi breaks through the slope in **5 years**. Each lapsed ledger thread shortens this by 1 month. Each 50 births in the household lengthens it by 1 month. The Bridge, combined with the household's births, can halt it entirely. |
| **The Veil** | Follows the knot count (see 09). |
| **Kakuriyo politics** | The vacant 100th seat in the Ie-no-Ma is contested by the House of Kamaitachi, a Hōrai-backed merchant clan and (potentially) **House Itokake**. A Diet vote is scheduled for the spring after the MC's arrival. |

## 4. Arc Outline: The First Year

A suggested shape. All arcs can be reordered, merged or skipped.

### Arc 1: "The 108 Steps" (Hachiōji; weeks 1–4)
The MC prays and is transformed. Tsumugi wakes. Inaba arrives. The MC learns about the threads and the clock, the hard way. **Kaito** at the konbini, **Shinga** in the antique shop, **Aoi** tailing the MC, **Ran** and the Shioya. The first fights against Mushi and a **Hōmu-gui** at Hachiōji Station. The first knot. The Mayoiga moves in. The first pregnancy and the first birth. A glimpse of **the Unclaimed** at Kōyōdai Danchi.

### Arc 2: "Crossing the Wani" (weeks 4–8)
Inaba teaches **Wani-watari**, and the MC meets the wani (and hears about Shirozame). Arrival in **Itokake-chō**: **Ayame** waiting at the shrine. **Hayate** arrests the MC. **Tamaki** streams the MC's first Hyakki fight and it goes viral. **Kyū** arrives with a clipboard. A licensing hearing at the Kebiishi-chō. Kakuriyo learns what the MC is.

### Arc 3: "Sanya" (weeks 6–12)
The Kamidomari and **Funado**, who tells the MC about the Wager and the crack in the boulder. **Satsuki, Mikuji, Izumi** and **Midori**. The MC's first Kodoku. The **Kirigami-shū** makes its first cut. **Danjō**'s Kodoku hounds.

### Arc 4: "Kurogane" (months 3–5)
**Haruto** on the last train. **Kurogane Holdings** turns out to be an Ōmagatsu. **Shūden** in the Labyrinth beneath Shinjuku Station. **Gōda**'s squad is sent after the MC. The first clue to the **kegare trade**.

### Arc 5: "Lantern and Fox" (months 3–6)
Dinner with **Akane** at the top of the Inari tower. **Tamamo** gets to the MC first. **Seika** tries to bind the MC. The **Grace market** opens. The idea of **House Itokake**. A hearing at the Yagyō-in. **Mayu**, **Ilaria**, **Konrad**, **Kasane**, **Ren**, **Mame** and **Dr. Yumemi** enter the household's orbit.

### Arc 6: "Tanabata" (July 7)
The Seal weakens for a night. **Orihime** speaks to the MC through Tsumugi. Threads light at 100 meters, and several light at once. The Jingikan guards every shrine. **The Kirigami-shū's major strike.** **Hizuki Rin** faces the household.

### Arc 7: "Obon and the Night Parade" (August)
The dead return. Yomi's traffic peaks. The hags come up from the subway. **Kuro-ikazuchi** blacks out Tokyo. The household marches in the **Hyakki Yagyō Festival**.

### Arc 8: "Kisaragi Station" (September)
The MC's late train stops at a station that doesn't exist. **Yoru** and **Masako**. **Fusu-ikazuchi** at the border. **Tsukimi** with Inaba.

### Arc 9: "Kamiarizuki" (November)
The household travels to **Izumo**. Utsushiyo's shrines are empty and its Magatsu surge. The **Kamiari Council** hears the matter of the Severance. **Tachikiri** argues against the MC. The MC demands seats for the Yadonashi-gami. **Sukunabikona**'s sake is due.

### Arc 10: "The 108th Toll" (December 31)
The Jingikan's Seal rite beneath **Sōmusubi Jingū**. **Project Ito-bashira.** The Itomaki Vault, and **A-gyō**. Whether the MC recovers the spindle, interrupts the rite or is taken.

### Later Arcs (Year 2 and Beyond)
- **"The Inaba Sea":** Shirozame, Otohime and Ryūgū; Inaba's skin and thread; Inaba's choice.
- **"House Itokake":** the vote for the 100th seat.
- **"The Kegare Trade":** exposing the Jingikan–Inari arrangement; the fall of the Lantern Party government; Akane's choice.
- **"The Unclaimed":** the mass re-tying at Kōyōdai Danchi (54 knots).
- **"Enjō":** the household becomes public in Utsushiyo, and the internet turns on them.
- **"The Eight Thunders":** Yomi's generals come up.
- **"The Bridge":** the 108th knot.

## 5. Endgame

### The Bridge
At 108 knots (or fewer plus the Itomaki, if threads have lapsed), the Shimenawa unravels into **the Bridge** (see 09): the realms reconnected, the 1.2 million severed threads released, the Veil failing over three years, reiatsu equalizing, and Kakuriyo's predators crossing to fight the infestation.

### The Renewed Wager
Yomi's crack does not close by itself. The deepest ending lies at **Yomotsu Hirasaka** in Izumo, at the Chibiki-iwa:
- The MC is musubi in one body: generation, Izanagi and Izanami together.
- At the boulder, the MC can **renew the Wager**: speak Izanagi's vow again in their own name.
- With the Bridge built, **the births of both realms count together**, and the MC's household's births are counted too. If births outnumber deaths in the combined count, the boulder reseals.
- **Izanami** can be spoken with. What she wants is acknowledgment, and perhaps for Izanagi to answer her at last. The MC may be able to bring Izanagi out of his silence on Awaji.
- **The Eight Thunders** stand in the way, with **Saku-ikazuchi** above all, which wants the MC's fertility ended.

### The MC's Apotheosis
At 108 knots the MC is Kami-class. With Itokake Shrine fully restored and worshipped, the MC can **become a kami of musubi**, Tsumugi's successor or partner. This is a choice, not a requirement.

### Failure Endings
| Ending | Cause |
|---|---|
| **The Pillar** | Ito-bashira succeeds. The MC sleeps bound in the vault; the Seal stands for centuries; the partners live without the MC. |
| **The Flood** | The Seal is torn instead of unspun (the Itomaki is destroyed, or Danzetsu at the hub). A reiatsu flood strikes Utsushiyo. |
| **The Slope Opens** | Yomi breaks through before the Wager is renewed. |
| **Unraveled** | Enough threads lapse that the Bridge cannot be made, and the Itomaki is lost. |
