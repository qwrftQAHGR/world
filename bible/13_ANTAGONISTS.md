# 13. Antagonists and Threats

Antagonists are listed roughly from the most immediate to the most distant. Many can be redeemed, negotiated with or turned. Some are on the ledger.

---

## 1. The Kirigami-shū (切り神衆): The Bond-Cutters

### Origin
In 2019, **Kiriya Sōgen**, captain of the Jingikan's Hasami-gumi (Scissors Unit), defected with eleven of his cutters. For twenty years his job had been cutting cross-realm threads that slipped past the Seal. He came to believe the Jingikan was too weak and compromised (by the Inari kegare deal) to protect Japan, and that the Seal was failing. He founded the Kirigami-shū to protect the severance by other means.

### Beliefs
- **Bonds can be poison.** Every member has suffered from a bond: an abusive parent, a violent spouse, a grief that would not let go, an addiction. Tachikiri's shears freed them. They believe cutting is mercy.
- **The Seal saves lives.** If the worlds reconnect, a reiatsu flood will kill thousands, yokai will prey on humans, and the Magatsu will multiply. (They are partly right: a disorderly opening would do this.)
- **The MC is a catastrophe.** A mortal holding 108 threads is an unnatural knot. They believe it will strangle the MC, kill the partners when threads lapse, and tear the Seal open. **Cutting the MC's threads is the merciful thing,** even if the partners die.
- Some members genuinely believe they are saving the MC from a burden that will destroy them.

### Structure
- **About 40 cutters**: trained thread-cutters with enkiri blades. The original 12 defectors plus recruits trained since 2019.
- **About 300 lay followers**: people who came to the group's free "severance counseling" sessions, held for victims of abusive relationships and addiction. Most lay followers do not know about the violence. Many were genuinely helped.
- **Ranks:** *Kiri-te* (cutter), *Hasami-gashira* (shears-captain, leading a cell of five), *Daiseki* (high seat; Kiriya and his three lieutenants).

### Bases
- **Headquarters:** the **Hall of Eternal Vows** (Eien no Chikai Kaikan), a closed-down wedding hall in Shinjuku's Nishi-Ōkubo district: a six-story 1980s building with a faux-European chapel, a Shinto wedding room and banquet halls. The group bought it in 2020 when its operator went bankrupt. The chapel is now its temple to Tachikiri; the banquet halls are its training rooms.
- **Counseling centers:** three storefront "severance counseling" offices in Shinjuku, Ikebukuro and Hachiōji.
- **A mountain retreat** in Shiga near a ruined enkiri shrine, used for training and rites.

### Leadership
**Kiriya Sōgen (桐谷宗玄)**, 52. High Seat.
- **Appearance:** tall, lean, shaved head, a neat grey beard, a scar across his left hand where a thread once burned him. Wears a black samue (Buddhist work clothes) and a long coat. Carries **Tachibasami**, a pair of meter-long iron shears blessed by Tachikiri herself.
- **History:** he was born with a cross-realm red thread, pulled into the Seal at birth. He has lived with the hollow feeling all his life. His wife left him; his daughter died of illness at 12. He cut his own grief with Tachikiri's shears and was freed, and has believed in cutting ever since.
- **Personality:** calm, articulate, sorrowful, utterly certain. He does not hate the MC; he pities them.
- **Power:** a master cutter, the best in Japan. He can perform **Danzetsu** (see 12) at a range of 40 meters. He fights with the shears, which can cut steel, spells and knotted threads.
- **Goal:** to cut every one of the MC's knots and threads before the Seal fails, then protect the Seal permanently.
- **Weak point:** he does not know that his own orphaned thread is one of the 40,000 in the Shimenawa, and that its other end belonged to a woman in Kakuriyo who died waiting. If he learned this, it might break him or change him.

### Lieutenants
- **Hizuki Rin (火月凛)**, 23. A former top student at Kanbe Academy (the Jingikan's seer school) who defected with Kiriya. Short black hair, sharp features, wears a dark sailor-style coat. Cold, precise and fast; she fights with twin enkiri tantō. She cut the bond to her abusive mother at 16, with Kiriya's help, and owes him her life. **Possible redemption:** she has never seen a good bond up close. Being around the MC's household could change her.
- **Tome-baa (留婆)**, 84. An old priestess of a ruined enkiri shrine in Kyoto, the group's spiritual authority. Tiny, bent, sharp-tongued, smokes a long kiseru pipe. She performs the cutting rites and blesses the blades. She sincerely believes 108 threads will strangle the MC, and she is grandmotherly toward the MC even while trying to cut them free.
- **Danjō (断丈)**, 40s. A disgraced exorcist who has learned to **tame Kodoku** and keeps a pack of them as hounds on grey leashes. Silent, gaunt, always in a grey raincoat. The most dangerous of the three, and the least sympathetic. He does not believe in anything; he enjoys watching bonds die.

### Tactics
Ambush lit, unknotted partners and cut their threads to drop the clock to 7 days. Use Danzetsu to break up the household's coordination. Turn the MC's partners against the MC by "counseling" them. Send Danjō's Kodoku against pregnant partners. Recruit the grieving.

### Tachikiri's Position
The goddess Tachikiri has neither endorsed nor stopped the group. She is troubled by them. If the MC can convince her, she can withdraw her blessing from their blades, which halves their power. She is also on the ledger (see 14).

## 2. The Jingikan Hardliners

### Director Hakuō Tadanori
See 03. He wants the MC **contained**: sealed, imprisoned at Sekisho, or kept under Jingikan control.

### Mibu Sachiko, Head of the Rites Bureau
See 03. Mibu has a plan of her own, which she has not told the Director: **Project Ito-bashira (糸柱, "Thread Pillar").**
- In Japanese folklore, a **hitobashira** (human pillar) was a person buried alive in the foundation of a bridge or castle to make it stand.
- Mibu's plan: capture the MC, take them to the Itomaki Vault, and **wind the MC's 108 threads onto the Itomaki**, making the MC a living pillar of the Seal. The MC's power would reinforce the Shimenawa for centuries. The MC would remain alive, unconscious and bound in the vault, permanently.
- The ledger partners would remain anchored to the MC through the spindle, and their clocks would stop. They would live, but would never meet the MC again.
- Mibu considers this humane, since no one dies.
- **Resources:** the Faith Grid, the loyal remainder of the Hasami-gumi, and about 200 Harae-tai operators under her influence.

### The Hasami-gumi (Loyal Remainder)
About 28 cutters who did not defect, now led by **Captain Ōnuma Sei (大沼静)**, 39: precise, quiet, by-the-book, and privately sick of the work. Assigned to cut the MC's threads under Jingikan control. Ōnuma is a possible defector to the MC's side.

### Deputy Director Kurokawa Isao
See 03. Not an antagonist. He thinks the Seal is failing, and he is the person in the Jingikan most likely to open a back channel to the MC.

## 3. The Inari Secret: The Kegare Trade

### The Arrangement
- **Since 1987,** the Jingikan's Rites Bureau has collected kegare from Utsushiyo's Magatsu nests and compressed it into sealed ceramic jars called **kegare-dama** (穢れ玉, "kegare balls"), each the size of a sake cask.
- **Sources:** the Harae-tai's exterminations; the Ōmagatsu Kurogane, which sells its harvested kegare to the Rites Bureau directly; and "seeded" nests the Rites Bureau has deliberately left uncleared.
- **Shipment:** the jars cross through the **Kanmon** under Sōmusubi Jingū. The Treaty of the Closed Door limits *envoys*, not cargo.
- **Refining:** in Kakuriyo, **Kitsunebi Power Plant No. 9** in western Musashi refines the kegare into fuel. It supplies about 5% of Kakuriyo's electricity, and that share is rising.
- **Payment:** the Inari Group pays the Rites Bureau in **Shinkō bonds**, which keep the Faith Grid supplied and the Jingikan's exorcists powered.

### Who Knows
- **Jingikan:** Director Hakuō, Mibu, and about a dozen Rites Bureau officials.
- **Inari:** the Myōbu Council, including Chair Kuzunoha Akane.
- **Tamamo-no-Mae**, who found out within weeks of arriving in Kakuriyo.

### What It Means
- **Both the Jingikan and the Inari Group profit from the infestation.** The Jingikan needs the Shinkō. Inari needs the cheap fuel.
- The Rites Bureau has deliberately left some nests uncleared to keep the supply flowing. Some of the deaths caused by those Magatsu are on the Jingikan's hands.
- If exposed in Kakuriyo, it would bring down the Lantern Party government. If exposed in the Jingikan, it would split the office.

### The Inari Factions
- **Kuzunoha Akane** (see 14), the Chair, inherited the deal from her predecessor in 2001. She dislikes it, considers it necessary for Kakuriyo's energy supply, and keeps it going. She is looking for a way out.
- **Yakō Senri (夜行千里)**, a six-tailed Myōbu Council member and head of Kitsunebi Power, wants to **expand** the trade. He is cold, ambitious and Akane's main rival on the council. He would like the MC captured and used. He is a likely ally of Mibu's Ito-bashira plan.

## 4. Tamamo-no-Mae (玉藻前)

- **Who:** the most notorious fox in Japanese legend. In the 12th century she was the beloved courtesan of Emperor Toba, until the onmyōji Abe no Yasunari (of the Abe line) exposed her as a nine-tailed fox. She fled, was hunted down at Nasu, and turned into the **Sesshō-seki**, the Killing Stone, which gave off poison for 850 years.
- **Freed:** in March 2022, the Killing Stone split in two (as it did in reality). She walked out. Within the month she crossed to Kakuriyo through a Mujina-michi tunnel.
- **Rank:** Kishin, with **nine tails**. Nine tails would normally mean Kami rank, but she has no shrine and no worshippers.
- **Appearance:** tall, with a heart-shaped face, gold eyes, long pale-gold hair, and nine golden tails. She dresses in expensive modern fashion with a classical touch: a tailored kimono-style coat, heels and jade. She looks about 30.
- **Personality:** charming, playful, cruel when it suits her, very patient, and very clever. Funny. Bitter about 850 years in a stone. Contemptuous of the Inari Group's "shopkeepers."
- **Goals:**
  1. **Become a kami.** She needs a shrine with worship and 8 K of blessing.
  2. **Take the Inari Group.** She believes the kitsune have become corporate drones and that she should rule them.
  3. **Revenge on the Abe line**, the house that exposed her (Abe no Seika is its heir; see 14).
- **Methods:** seduction, blackmail (she knows about the kegare trade), illusion, patience.
- **Toward the MC:** she sees the MC as the fastest path to godhood. She is on the ledger (see 14). She would very much like to be knotted, for her own reasons, and she would like the MC's other partners not to matter.
- **Threat level:** high. One of the most powerful beings in Kakuriyo.

## 5. The Thread-Forgers

- **Ōgumo Shizuri (大蜘蛛静理)**, a jorōgumo of about 300 years and an Ōyōkai, and the best forger of red threads in Kakuriyo.
- **Workshop:** a hidden loft in the back alleys of Kumo-machi, Hyakki, behind a shop selling cheap silk scarves.
- **Business:** forged red threads, sold to Houses and corporations who want an agent in the MC's household. Also sells counterfeit Grace marks and fake Knot marks.
- **Clients:** Yakō Senri (Inari), the Snow Coalition, an Ashiya-ryū contractor and a Hōrai merchant house.
- **Personal:** she is **Ayame's estranged younger sister** (see 14). She left Itokake-chō two centuries ago after a quarrel over a man and has never returned. Ayame still keeps a loom for her.
- **Possible arc:** exposing her network, or recruiting her to forge protection instead of deception.

## 6. Shirozame (白鮫): The White-skin Wani

- **Who:** a wani-lord of the **Inaba Sea**, about 4,000 years old, a Kishin. He was the last wani in the line that Inaba crossed in the myth: the one who caught the hare and stripped its skin.
- **Appearance:** in his humanoid form, a huge pale man over 2.5 meters tall with grey shark-skin, black eyes, rows of teeth and gill-slits at his neck. **Over his shoulders he wears a cape of white rabbit fur that glows faintly: Inaba's skin.**
- **Domain:** a sea-fortress of coral and wrecked ships beneath the Inaba Sea. He commands the wani of the Inaba coast and collects tolls on every Wani-watari crossing in the region.
- **Inaba's thread:** the red thread that went with Inaba's skin is still attached to the cape. For four thousand years, Shirozame has felt it pull toward someone. He has never followed it. He has his own reasons.
- **Personality:** proud, petty, and full of grudges, but bound by the sea's laws of tolls and bargains. He keeps his word exactly.
- **Toward Inaba:** contempt on the surface. Underneath, something more complicated.
- **Toward the MC:** the MC's household crosses on his wani. He sets the toll. He will want something.
- **Possible arcs:** winning Inaba's skin back by bargain, contest or force. Discovering where Inaba's thread leads. Inaba's choice between thread and service (see 08).
- *(Where Inaba's thread leads is deliberately left open. Possibilities: someone in the household; someone on the ledger, which would create an unusual triangle; Shirozame himself; or someone long dead.)*

## 7. Yomi

### The Situation
- The **Wager** at the Chibiki-iwa: as long as births outnumber deaths, the boulder holds.
- Since 2005 deaths have exceeded births in Utsushiyo, and Kakuriyo's yokai births have collapsed. **The boulder is cracking.**
- **In 2026,** the crack is a hand's width wide. Funado feels it. The Hirasaka Watch in Izumo reports that cold air and whispers come through it at night.
- **When the crack is wide enough**, Yomi's forces can come up through the slope itself, not just seep through kegare pools. The Hirasaka Watch estimates this will happen within **five years** at the current rate.

### Izanami (伊邪那美)
- **State:** asleep beyond the boulder, slowly waking as the Wager turns.
- **Appearance (if seen):** a woman of enormous size, half her body beautiful and half rotted, with the eight thunders writhing in her flesh.
- **What she wants:** the Wager has always been her grief speaking. She wanted Izanagi to stay. She wants to win, to be acknowledged, to not be the one who was left in the dark.
- **Relationship to the MC:** the MC embodies both Izanagi and Izanami, the generative pair made whole. She feels the MC's births from the bottom of Yomi. She is **interested** in the MC.
- **Not an enemy to be killed.** She is a god of creation and death. The endgame confrontation with Yomi can end in negotiation or a renewed Wager (see 15).

### The Eight Thunders (Yakusa-no-Ikazuchi, 八雷神)
Eight thunder gods born from the parts of Izanami's rotting body. Yomi's generals. Each is roughly as powerful as a Kishin, and each has a domain.

| Thunder | From | Domain | Status in 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ō-ikazuchi** (大雷, Great Thunder) | Head | Command, overwhelming force | In Yomi. The leader. |
| **Ho-no-ikazuchi** (火雷, Fire Thunder) | Breast | Fire, wildfire | In Yomi |
| **Kuro-ikazuchi** (黒雷, Black Thunder) | Belly | Darkness, blackouts | **Active in Tokyo since 2025.** Lives in the subway power grid. Causes blackouts, works with the hags, herds Magatsu. |
| **Saku-ikazuchi** (析雷, Splitting Thunder) | Genitals | Splitting, sundering, **barrenness** | In Yomi. **The MC's natural nemesis**: where the MC generates, it splits; where the MC makes fertile, it makes barren. It wants the MC's fertility ended. |
| **Waka-ikazuchi** (若雷, Young Thunder) | Left hand | Speed, sudden strikes | In Yomi |
| **Tsuchi-ikazuchi** (土雷, Earth Thunder) | Right hand | Earth, the ground opening | In Yomi |
| **Naru-ikazuchi** (鳴雷, Roaring Thunder) | Left foot | Sound, terror | In Yomi |
| **Fusu-ikazuchi** (伏雷, Lurking Thunder) | Right foot | Stealth, ambush | **Seen at Kisaragi Station**, testing the border |

### The Yomotsu-shikome (Hags of Yomi)
See 04 and 12. In 2026, about 30 hags operate in Tokyo's subway tunnels under Kuro-ikazuchi, collecting stuck souls and herding Magatsu.

### Yomi's Goals
1. **Kill the MC.** The MC's births are the strongest counterweight to Yomi's side of the Wager. Saku-ikazuchi wants to end the MC's fertility; the others simply want the MC dead.
2. **Widen the crack** in the Chibiki-iwa.
3. **Collect souls** stuck in transit, to strengthen Yomi.
4. **Break the Wager** and open the slope.
