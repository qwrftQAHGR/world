# 02. Metaphysics and Magic

## 1. The Structure of a Soul: Ichirei Shikon (One Spirit, Four Souls)

Every living being with a spirit (humans, beastfolk, yokai, ijin and kami) has the same structure. It is taken from real Shinto theology.

### The Naobi (直霊): the Core
The single governing spirit: the self, conscience and will. The Naobi holds the four souls in balance and decides what the being does with its power. When the Naobi is darkened by kegare, it becomes **magatsuhi**, a corrupting spirit, and the being's soul begins to rot (see Kegare below).

### The Four Mitama (御魂)

| Mitama | Nature | Powers it governs | Personality when dominant |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aramitama** (荒魂) | Rough, violent, active | Physical strength, speed, combat, elemental force (wind, fire, lightning), raw destruction, courage | Bold, impulsive, protective, quick to anger |
| **Nigimitama** (和魂) | Gentle, harmonious | Healing, barriers, purification, calming, growth, binding through peace | Patient, nurturing, steady, conflict-averse |
| **Sakimitama** (幸魂) | Fortune | Luck, probability, prosperity, blessings and curses, fertility, abundance | Generous, lucky, cheerful, sometimes careless |
| **Kushimitama** (奇魂) | Wondrous, mysterious | Illusion, shapeshifting, divination, kotodama, memory, transformation, insight | Curious, clever, secretive, strange |

### The Soul Profile
Every being has a **profile**: a distribution across the four mitama, written as four numbers adding to 10. Examples:
- An ordinary human: about **3/3/2/2** (Ara/Nigi/Saki/Kushi). Balanced and weak in all four.
- A karasu-tengu: **5/2/1/2**. Built for combat.
- A kitsune: **2/1/2/5**. Illusion and transformation.
- A kappa: **3/3/1/3**. Strong, healing, clever.
- A zashiki-warashi (luck house spirit): **0/2/7/1**.
- An oni: **6/1/2/1**.
- A jorōgumo: **2/2/1/5**.
- A yuki-onna: **3/2/0/5**.

**Imbalance:** when one mitama reaches 7 or more, it starts to spill over and dominate the being's personality and behavior. An Ara-7 oni is prone to rampages. A Kushi-7 kitsune can lose track of their true form. Imbalance is natural for some species and dangerous for all.

Profiles shift with experience, training and blessings. The MC's blessings can raise individual mitama (see 10).

## 2. The Fuels

Mitama are what a soul **does**. A fuel is what it **runs on**. Every spiritual being draws on one or more of these.

### Osore (畏れ): Fear and Awe
The oldest fuel. When a human fears or stands in awe of a being, that being is fed. Osore is:
- **Strong:** a single terrified human gives more than a hundred faithful worshippers.
- **Cheap:** easy to generate. Startle someone in the dark.
- **Unstable:** every use of Osore produces **1.5×** the normal kegare.
- **Human-sourced:** only humans generate significant Osore. Beastfolk and yokai generate very little. This is why the Seal starved Kakuriyo: its main source of Osore was cut off.

Urban-legend yokai (toshi-densetsu) live almost entirely on Osore. Magatsu feed on the negative emotions that produce it.

### Shinkō (信仰): Faith and Attention
The fuel of the gods. When a being is worshipped, prayed to, remembered or simply paid sincere attention, it is fed. Shinkō is:
- **Stable:** standard kegare (1×).
- **Storable:** it accumulates in shrines, statues, talismans and sacred objects. A shrine is a battery of Shinkō built up by generations of worship.
- **Poolable:** it can be gathered from many people into one place and drawn out by priests.

The Jingikan's control of the great shrines means it controls most of Utsushiyo's stored Shinkō. Every licensed exorcist and enshrined god draws from that supply (see 03, the Faith Grid). In Kakuriyo, Shinkō is a traded commodity (see 05, Shinkō bonds).

### En (縁): Bonds
The fuel of connection: love, friendship, family, loyalty, partnership. Every bond produces a small amount of En, which normally flows invisibly between the people bonded and keeps them healthy and lucky. En is:
- **Slow:** it accumulates over time and cannot be generated quickly.
- **Clean:** using En produces **no kegare**.
- **Inexhaustible:** a bond is not used up by drawing on it. It is strengthened.
- **Inaccessible:** before the MC, no one could draw on En deliberately as a fuel. The MC is the first being since the Age of the Gods able to gather and use it.

The MC's knots, blessings, children and household generate En constantly. The MC's own powers run on it (see 12).

### Comparison

| Fuel | Source | Strength | Kegare per use | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Osore | Human fear | High | 1.5× | Yokai, toshi-densetsu, Magatsu |
| Shinkō | Worship, attention | Medium | 1× | Kami, priests, onmyōji, licensed exorcists |
| En | Bonds | Low per moment, grows | 0× | The MC; the MC's partners via the MC |
| Innate | A being's own spirit | Low | 1× | Everyone, for small things |

Every spiritual being also has a small **innate** reserve, enough for minor abilities (a kitsune's small illusions, a kappa's water sense). Larger abilities require a fuel.

## 3. Kegare (穢れ): Spiritual Pollution

Kegare is the residue of spiritual activity and of negative emotion. It accumulates in beings, objects and places.

### Sources of Kegare
- **Using power.** Every technique fueled by Osore, Shinkō or innate reserves leaves kegare in the user. Aramitama techniques leave double.
- **Negative emotion.** Despair, rage, envy, exhaustion and loneliness produce kegare in the person feeling them. Ordinary humans produce a steady trickle.
- **Death and blood.** Contact with death, violence and corpses produces kegare.
- **Contact with Magatsu.** Being struck or touched by a Magatsu transfers kegare.
- **Yomi.** Anything from Yomi is saturated with kegare.

### The Kegare Scale
Kegare in a being is measured from 0 to 100.

| Range | Stage | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| 0–19 | **Clean** (Kiyome) | No effects. |
| 20–39 | **Smudged** (Kegare) | Fatigue, minor bad luck, irritability, poor sleep. Mushi are drawn to the person. |
| 40–59 | **Stained** (Yogore) | Techniques cost more. Strong emotions are hard to control. Visible to those with sight as a grey haze around the body. |
| 60–79 | **Fouled** (Kegare-tsuki) | The soul profile begins to warp toward its dominant mitama. **Ara:** rage, violence. **Nigi:** apathy, smothering control. **Saki:** misfortune spreading to people nearby. **Kushi:** hallucinations, losing track of what is real. Tsukimono can latch on. |
| 80–99 | **Corrupted** (Magatsuhi) | The Naobi darkens into magatsuhi. The being starts acting against its own will and values. Physical signs: dark veins, eyes losing color, a smell of rot. |
| 100 | **Fallen** (Ochimono) | The being becomes a Magatsu, or, if its bonds were severed, a Kodoku. In most cases this is irreversible. |

### Typical Kegare Costs
- A minor technique (a small illusion, a talisman): 1
- A standard combat technique: 3–5
- A major technique (a building-sized barrier, a lightning strike): 10–15
- An all-out ultimate technique: 20–30
- A hit from a Tier-3 Magatsu: 5–10
- A day of ordinary despair: 1–2

### Purification (Harae)
Kegare is removed by **purification**.

| Method | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Shrine visit** (a ritual cleansing at an active shrine) | –20 |
| **Misogi** (ritual bathing under a waterfall or in the sea) | –20; –30 at a sacred site |
| **Ōharae** (the Great Purification, held June 30 and December 31) | –40 for everyone attending; lowers kegare in places |
| **Salt** | Removes kegare from objects and places; scattered salt drives off Mushi |
| **Sincere laughter** | –5. In myth, the laughter of the gods drew Amaterasu out of her cave. |
| **The MC's Hearth** (any sincere union with the MC) | **Complete cleansing to 0** for both participants |
| **The MC's milk** | –20; also heals |
| **Tachibana fruit** (from Tokoyo) | –30; also heals |

The MC's Hearth is the most powerful purification method in existence, and it repeats. This makes the MC's household the only group that can fight constantly without slowly corrupting.

## 4. Spiritual Atmosphere (Reiatsu)

The density of spiritual energy in a place is called **reiatsu**.

| Place | Reiatsu | Effect on yokai | Effect on human magic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Utsushiyo city | Very thin | 50% power | Normal |
| Utsushiyo active shrine, forest, sacred mountain | Thin | 75% power | +25% |
| Utsushiyo kegare nest | Thin but foul | 50% power, +kegare | Normal, +kegare |
| Kakuriyo city | Dense | 100% | ×2 |
| Kakuriyo sacred site | Very dense | 125% | ×3, and reiyoi |
| Yomi border | Dense and foul | 100%, +5 kegare per hour | ×2, +5 kegare per hour |

**Anchors:** a yokai in Utsushiyo regains full power in a place where it is believed in or feared. A kappa at a river with a kappa legend and a warning sign is at full strength. Kuchisake-onna is at full strength anywhere people tell her story.

**The MC as anchor:** the MC's partners carry the MC's En. In Utsushiyo they operate at **75%** anywhere, and at **100%** within about 100 meters of the MC.

**Reiyoi (spirit-drunkenness):** humans arriving in Kakuriyo experience headaches, euphoria, heightened senses and slight intoxication for 2–6 hours. Repeated crossings build tolerance.

## 5. Perception and Sight

Seeing the spiritual is called **mieru** ("to see").

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| Ordinary humans | Nothing. They feel chills, dread or moods. The Veil explains away anything that gets through. |
| Very young children (under about 4) | Glimpses. They often cry at "nothing." |
| Cats, and some dogs, crows and foxes | Most Ura beings. Cats hunt Mushi. |
| **Mieru-hito** (about 1 in 10,000 humans) | Everything in the Ura. Born with it. Seeing a Magatsu means it notices you. |
| Awakened humans (after the MC's Knot) | Everything in the Ura. |
| Yokai, kami, ijin | Everything. |
| The MC (thread-sight) | Everything, **plus bonds** (see below). |
| The MC's children | Everything, plus bonds. |

### Thread-Sight (Ito-mi)
The MC and the MC's children can see **bonds** as colored threads running from the left little finger of each person to the people they are bonded with. The threads pass through walls and stretch to any distance, fading from view after about 50 meters unless the MC concentrates.

| Color | Bond |
|---|---|
| **Red** | Destined romantic bond (fate). Only ledger threads tied to the MC, and the few uncut destined threads in the world, appear red. |
| **Pink** | Romantic love that is not fated |
| **White** | Family: parent, child, sibling, chosen family |
| **Gold** | Friendship |
| **Blue** | Duty, obligation, loyalty, contracts, vows |
| **Black** | Rivalry, hatred, grudges |
| **Silver** | A bond to a god: worship, a shrine's parishioners, a messenger's service |
| **Grey, frayed** | A severed bond still trailing from the person |
| **Grey, snarled** | Kodoku threads |
| **Red, with a burning tip** | A lit ledger thread with its 49-day clock running |

Thread thickness shows strength. A newly formed friendship is a hair; a lifelong marriage is a cord.

## 6. Ranks: Kaku (格)

Kakuriyo officially ranks all spiritual beings by spiritual weight. The rank appears on Kakuriyo ID cards and determines legal rights, voting weight in the Yagyō-in and licensing.

| Rank | Title | Description | Approximate share of Kakuriyo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5** | **Kotsu** (小つ, "small one") | Ordinary beastfolk, minor yokai, most humans. Small innate abilities. | 70% |
| **4** | **Yōkai** (妖怪) | Full yokai. Real innate powers, long lifespans (200–1,000 years). | 25% |
| **3** | **Ōyōkai** (大妖怪, great yokai) | Clan heads, police chiefs, famous figures. Powers able to affect a city block. | 4.5% |
| **2** | **Kishin** (鬼神, demon-god) | Living legends. Powers able to affect a district. A few hundred in all of Kakuriyo. | 0.5% |
| **1** | **Kami** (神) | Gods. Require a shrine and worshippers. | (counted separately) |

### Visible Rank Markers
Many species show their rank in their bodies.

| Species | Yōkai | Ōyōkai | Kishin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kitsune | 1–2 tails | 3–5 tails | 6–8 tails (nine is legendary) |
| Tengu | Crow features, small black wings | Larger wings, longer nose begins | Full red-faced daitengu, long nose, great wings |
| Oni | 1 horn | 2 horns | 2 horns plus a third, smaller horn |
| Nekomata | Tail split near the tip | Tail split halfway | Fully forked tail, burning-ghost-fire tips |
| Jorōgumo | 2 spider legs from the back | 4 legs | 8 legs, full spider lower body available |
| Kappa | Plain shell | Patterned shell | Shell glows; head-dish never runs dry |
| Tanuki | Ordinary shapeshifting | Can shapeshift others | Can shapeshift places |
| Yuki-onna | Frost breath | Breath freezes a room | Breath freezes a street; snow follows them |

### Promotion
Historically, beings rose in rank through age, faith and fear: a cat that lived long enough became a nekomata, a fox gained tails over centuries, a shrine's worship raised a spirit into a kami. Since the Seal cut off human faith and fear in 1914, promotion has nearly stopped. Kakuriyo records only a few dozen natural promotions per year.

**The MC's blessings promote beings reliably** (see 10). This makes the MC the most politically significant person in Kakuriyo.

### Rank Thresholds in Blessing Units
Blessings are measured in **K** (one full Knot blessing). Promotion costs:

| Promotion | Cost |
|---|---|
| Kotsu → Yōkai | 1 K |
| Yōkai → Ōyōkai | 2 K |
| Ōyōkai → Kishin | 4 K |
| Kishin → Kami | 8 K, plus a shrine with active worship |

## 7. Shapes: Utsuri (移り)

Most yokai, awakened humans and many ijin have more than one form.

- **Human form (Hito-gata):** looks human or nearly human, with perhaps ears or a tail (mimi-gata). This is the default in Utsushiyo and costs nothing to maintain there.
- **True form (Hon-gata):** the being's full nature: wings, tails, spider legs, a serpent body, full fur. Maximum power. In Utsushiyo, holding true form drains innate reserve slowly and makes the being more visible to anything with sight.
- **Intermediate forms:** many beings can choose partial forms.

Switching takes a breath and is free. Awakened partners of the MC can switch at will once their blessing has unfolded (see 10).

**Henge (Disguise):** a separate technique, mainly for kitsune and tanuki, of taking a false shape that is not one's own. Henge costs kegare to maintain and can be broken by mirrors, dogs, sincere prayer or a strong blow. The MC's household relies on kitsune or tanuki henge when the MC's body becomes too conspicuous for Utsushiyo (see 11).

## 8. Kotodama (言霊): The Power of Words

Words carry spirit. Spoken sincerely, they act on the world.

- **Names.** Every being has an everyday name and a **true name (imina)**. Knowing a being's true name gives power over it: binding, summoning, cursing. Yokai and gods guard their true names. By tradition, a partner gives the MC their true name at the Knot.
- **Vows (chikai).** A sincere vow spoken aloud creates a blue thread of obligation. Breaking it produces kegare (+20) and bad luck.
- **Norito (prayers).** Formal Shinto prayers draw Shinkō from a shrine and direct it.
- **Kuji (nine syllables).** *Rin, Pyō, Tō, Sha, Kai, Jin, Retsu, Zai, Zen*: nine syllables with nine hand seals, used by onmyōji and yamabushi to cut a grid in the air that blocks and binds spirits.
- **Lies.** Lying in a sacred place produces kegare (+5). Lying while crossing the wani makes them bite.
- **Factory faith.** Mass-printed talismans work, but at about one third of the strength of handwritten ones, because no single person's intention went into them.

## 9. Magic in Utsushiyo: Technique Over Fuel

Human magic developed where spiritual fuel was scarce. It is precise, ritual-heavy and efficient, and it borrows power from shrines and sacred objects rather than generating it.

### Traditions
- **Onmyōdō (陰陽道).** The Way of Yin and Yang, practiced by the old court diviners. Talismans (**ofuda**, **gofu**), summoned paper servants (**shikigami**), divination, the five-pointed **Seiman** star (the seal of Abe no Seimei), the nine-by-nine **Dōman** grid (the seal of Ashiya Dōman), astrology and directional taboos.
- **Shugendō (修験道).** Mountain asceticism. The **yamabushi** train on sacred mountains through fasting, cold-water misogi and walking. They are famed for physical endurance, fire-walking and exorcism, and have an ancient connection to the tengu.
- **Miko arts.** The shrine maiden's arts: **kagura** sacred dance (draws and directs Shinkō), **suzu** bells (purifying sound), possession by a god for oracles.
- **Buddhist exorcism.** Sutras, prayer beads, mantras, the **dokko** (vajra). Strong against ghosts and Yomi creatures.
- **Folk magic.** Salt, peach wood, holly and sardine heads at doorways, mirrors, red thread and talismans from local shrines.

### Modern Methods
- **Salt rounds (shio-dan):** bullets with compressed purified-salt cores. Standard Jingikan ammunition.
- **Ofuda tape:** rolls of printed talismans, applied to weapons, doors and walls.
- **Shikigami drones:** small drones with a folded paper shikigami inside, used for scouting and spotting.
- **Suzu grenades:** bell-grenades whose purifying chime scatters Mushi and stuns Magatsu.
- **Kekkai stakes:** four engraved stakes that create a barrier field when planted in a square.
- **Seal apps:** smartphone apps that display seals. They work weakly (factory-faith rules) and are popular among young onmyōji.

### The Faith Grid
Utsushiyo's stored Shinkō sits in its large shrines. After the Shrine Consolidation, those shrines are mostly state-registered and their priests answer, unknowingly or knowingly, to the Jingikan's **Saishi-kyoku** (Rites Bureau). Licensed exorcists and enshrined gods draw power through the grid. **The Jingikan controls the supply of faith-power in Utsushiyo.** Anyone outside the grid (shrineless gods, unaffiliated seers, the MC) has to find their own fuel.

## 10. Magic in Kakuriyo: Fuel Over Technique

In Kakuriyo, power is abundant and innate. Techniques are less refined but far stronger, and the culture has industrialized them.

- **Innate arts.** Each species has its natural powers (see 07). Children learn them as they grow, the way human children learn to run.
- **Jutsu-kōgaku (術工学, spell engineering).** The fusion of magic and technology. Foxfire generators, spirit engines, silk data lines, ward-glass windows, Mushi-proof netting, Aramitama sidearms.
- **Licensing.** Kakuriyo licenses powers the way Utsushiyo licenses firearms and vehicles.

| Class | Covers | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **C** | Minor innate abilities: small illusions, keen senses, small flames | None |
| **B** | Combat-capable abilities | Registration with the tengu Kebiishi-chō |
| **A** | Area-effect or lethal Ara-class techniques | Permit, training, background check; city use only in emergencies |
| **S** | Kishin-level power | State oversight; movements reported |

Unlicensed use of A-class power in a city is a serious crime. The MC is unregistered and has no license of any class when they first arrive in Kakuriyo.

## 11. Death and the Dead

- **The 49 days.** When a being dies, its soul spends 49 days in transit, the **chūin**, on the road to Yomi's border, where it is judged and either passes on (to rebirth, or to become an ancestral spirit watching over its descendants) or lingers.
- **Yūrei (ghosts).** Souls that do not pass on after 49 days because of a grudge, attachment or violent death. Ghosts exist in the Ura in Utsushiyo and are visible in Kakuriyo. Since the Chibiki-iwa cracked in 2005, more souls have been getting stuck, and the ghost population has grown. Magatsu prey on ghosts.
- **Ikiryō (living ghosts).** Fragments of a living person's soul torn loose by intense emotion (usually jealousy or grudges), which act on their own.
- **Ancestral spirits (sorei).** Souls that passed on and watch over their family. They are strongest at Obon, when they return home.
- **Yomi's claim.** Anyone who eats Yomi's food belongs to Yomi. Anyone who falls to 100 kegare without bonds becomes Yomi's creature.
- **Resurrection does not exist.** No power in this setting, the MC's included, brings back the dead. Souls can be laid to rest, freed from Kodoku forms, or (rarely) reborn, but not restored.
