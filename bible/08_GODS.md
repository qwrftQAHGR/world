# 08. The Gods

## 1. How Kami Work

- **What a kami is:** a being of rank 1, sustained by **Shinkō** (faith). Kami range from great gods of the myths to minor gods of a single village well.
- **Shrines:** a kami's shrine is its home, its battery of stored Shinkō, and its anchor in the world. A kami with an active shrine and worshippers is stable. A kami without one fades.
- **Bunrei (分霊, divided spirits):** major kami can divide their spirit to inhabit many shrines at once. Inari has tens of thousands of shrines; each holds a fraction of Inari.
- **Visibility:** kami are invisible to ordinary people in Utsushiyo unless they choose to be seen, and even then people tend to forget meeting them. In Kakuriyo, kami can be seen by anyone but rarely appear in public.
- **Bodies:** a kami's body is made of Shinkō and can take whatever form the kami chooses, within the limits of its nature. A weak kami has a fragile, translucent body.
- **Power:** proportional to Shinkō. A well-worshipped regional kami is roughly as powerful as a Kishin. Great kami like Ōkuninushi are beyond any rank.
- **Registration:** in Utsushiyo, the Jingikan's **Enshrined Registry** licenses about 9,000 kami, who receive Shinkō stipends from the Faith Grid in exchange for obedience. In Kakuriyo, kami are registered at Izumo.
- **The 5-yen coin:** see 03. A sincerely given 5-yen coin carries En and feeds a kami for about a day.

## 2. Izumo and Ōkuninushi

### Ōkuninushi (大国主)
- **Role:** Lord of Hidden Matters. Sovereign of Kakuriyo in divine affairs. God of bonds, marriage and matchmaking (en-musubi), nation-building, medicine and agriculture.
- **Appearance:** when he appears, a tall broad man in white-and-indigo court robes with a large sack over one shoulder (as Daikokuten, with whom he is identified), a gentle round face and tired eyes. He appears in public once a year, at the opening of the Kamiari Council.
- **Personality:** patient, kind, weighed down. He has watched the bonds between the worlds rot for a century and could not stop it: the Seal was made by humans in the visible world, which he ceded to Amaterasu's line in the Land Transfer, and he has no authority there.
- **Stance on the MC:** he sent Inaba. He believes the MC is the answer to the Severance, but he is bound by the rules of the Land Transfer and must act through the Kamiari Council and his messengers rather than directly.

### The Great Shrine of Izumo
- In Kakuriyo, the Great Shrine stands **96 meters tall**, as legend says the original once did, on wooden pillars made of three bound cedar trunks each. A wooden staircase 109 meters long climbs to the main hall. It is the tallest wooden structure in either world.
- The compound includes the **Hare Office**, the council halls, lodging for 3,000 visiting gods (the **Jūkusha**, Nineteen Halls), and the archive of all registered bonds in Kakuriyo.

### The Hare Office (Usagi-dokoro, 兎所)
- Ōkuninushi's bureau of messengers and matchmakers, staffed by about **200 white hares**.
- Hares handle matchmaking cases, carry messages between gods, investigate broken bonds, and act as caseworkers on difficult fates.
- They wear white haori jackets marked with a double-wave crest and carry bamboo message tubes.
- The office is bureaucratic: forms, case numbers, quarterly reports. **Inaba** is its most senior hare and its most difficult employee.
- **Case file:** the MC is filed as **Hare Office Case No. 1909-0001, "The Itokake Tangle,"** opened in November 1909 and never closed.

### The Kamiari Council (神在祭)
In real Japanese tradition, the tenth month of the old calendar is called **Kannazuki**, "the month without gods," because all the gods of Japan leave their shrines and travel to Izumo. In Izumo alone it is called **Kamiarizuki**, "the month with gods." There they decide the bonds of the coming year: who will meet, marry and part.

In this setting:
- **When:** the tenth lunar month, roughly late October to late November. In 2026 the gods arrive on **November 19** (the 10th day of the 10th lunar month) and stay for seven days.
- **Arrival:** the gods land at night at **Inasa Beach**, welcomed by bonfires. Thousands of gods walk up from the sea in procession.
- **Attendance:** about 3,000 gods. Kami in both realms attend, in Kakuriyo's Izumo, since gods can still cross to it on this occasion. **Yadonashi-gami are not invited.** Since 1914, only gods with shrines have been seated.
- **The Kamihakari (神議り, divine deliberation):** the gods sit in council in the Nineteen Halls and decide the year's bonds. Each god speaks for its parishioners. The process is slow, ceremonial and political.
- **2026 agenda:** for the first time since 1914, "**the matter of the Severance**" is on the agenda. The gods will debate whether the Seal should be undone, and they will discuss the MC, whom some gods consider an abomination (a mortal holding 108 threads), some a miracle, and some a political tool. Every year from 2026 on, the MC's case is reviewed at the Kamiari Council.
- **The MC at the Council:** the MC can be summoned, or can petition to attend. A mortal speaking at the Kamiari Council has not happened in recorded history.

## 3. Tsumugi (紡): The Goddess of Itokake

- **Domain:** spinning, thread, silk, matchmaking and fertility. Keeper of the Itomaki, the spindle on which the cross-realm threads of the Musashi region were wound.
- **History:** worshipped at Itokake Shrine in Hachiōji from about 1150, during the period when the region became a silk-weaving center. For centuries the silk weavers of Hachiōji prayed to her for good harvests of silk and good marriages. Every bond between a person in Musashi and a person in the hidden Musashi passed across her spindle.
- **The loss:** in November 1909, Jingikan priests closed her shrine, confiscated the Itomaki and separated her komainu. Without the spindle or worshippers, she fell into a deep sleep that lasted 117 years, fading year by year.
- **The awakening:** when the MC prays at Itokake sincerely, Tsumugi wakes with almost nothing left. She uses the last of her strength on the **Futa-musubi rite** (see 09), making the MC the Musubi-bito and tying 108 orphaned threads to them.
- **Appearance:** a tall, slender adult woman with the features of a **silk moth**: skin white as raw silk, large black eyes with no whites, long feathery white antennae curving back from her brow like a crown, soft white down at her collar and wrists, and four broad white wings folded down her back like a cloak. Domesticated silk moths have lived with humans for 5,000 years and **can no longer fly or feed themselves**. Tsumugi's wings are the same: beautiful and unable to lift her. She wears a plain white kimono of raw silk.
- **Personality:** quiet, slow-spoken, gentle and deeply tired, with a dry humor. She speaks in an old-fashioned way and apologizes often. She feels intense guilt over what she did to the MC: she gave them power and an enormous burden without asking first, because there was no time. She cannot survive without care, and she knows it, and finds it humiliating.
- **Current state:** weak, translucent at the edges. At the start she **cannot leave the grounds of Itokake Shrine** (either version). She recovers as the MC ties knots and as the shrine receives worship:

| Knots | Tsumugi's state |
|---|---|
| 0 | Barely solid; can speak and perform small rites at the shrine |
| 9 | Solid; can perform full rites; can walk the 108 steps |
| 27 | Can leave the shrine and travel within Hachiōji and Itokake-chō |
| 54 | Can travel anywhere; can attend the Kamiari Council in her own right |
| 108 | Fully restored; her wings can carry her for the first time in five thousand years |

- **Abilities:** reading and spinning threads, the Futa-musubi rite, blessing marriages, blessing silk and harvests, seeing the 49-day clocks on every lit thread.
- **Not a ledger partner.** She is the one who tied the threads; she cannot hold one of her own to the MC.
- **Wants:** her spindle back. Her komainu reunited. Her shrine alive. The MC to forgive her.

## 4. Inaba (因幡): The White Hare

- **Identity:** the **White Hare of Inaba** from the Kojiki. In the myth, the hare tricked a line of wani (sea monsters) into lining up across the sea, claiming it wanted to count them, and hopped across their backs. On the last step it boasted of the trick, and the last wani caught it and stripped off its fur. Ōkuninushi's cruel brothers told the hare to bathe in seawater and lie in the wind, which made it worse. Ōkuninushi told it to wash in fresh water and roll in cattail pollen, and it healed. The hare then predicted that Ōkuninushi, not his brothers, would win the hand of Princess Yakami. It was the first act of matchmaking in Japanese myth.
- **Role:** senior messenger of the Hare Office. Caseworker on Case No. 1909-0001. Assigned to the MC as guide.
- **Age:** several thousand years.
- **Pronouns:** they/them, and they do not mind he or she. They answer to **Inaba** or **Hakuto** ("white hare").
- **Appearance:**
  - About 158 cm tall, slight and quick.
  - **Mimi-gata build** in public: a human body with tall white rabbit ears (which droop when sad and flatten when angry), a round white fluffy tail, and ruby-red eyes. Soft white fur at the wrists, ankles and the back of the neck.
  - **True form:** a white rabbit-person in full (zen-gata), or a large white hare.
  - **The scars:** faint hairless pink lines across their shoulders, back and arms where their skin was torn away. Visible when they wear anything without sleeves. They do not hide them.
  - **Clothing:** a white haori with the Hare Office's double-wave crest over whatever modern clothes they like (oversized hoodies, shorts, sneakers). A bamboo message tube on a cord. A small gourd of **cattail pollen** at their hip, the medicine Ōkuninushi gave them, which they use on others' wounds.
- **Personality:**
  - Talkative, fast, nosy, cheerful. Gossips about everyone, gods included.
  - A trickster. Loves pranks, bluffs and small harmless lies. **Has sworn never to lie about a thread**, and keeps the vow.
  - Constantly making or eating **mochi**. The rabbit in the moon pounds mochi, and Inaba considers it a family tradition.
  - Teases the MC mercilessly and protects them fiercely.
  - Underneath: lonely, carrying an old grief they do not talk about (see below), and deeply invested in other people's happiness because they cannot have their own.
- **Skills:**
  - **Counting Across (Kazoe-watari):** Inaba can cross any line of objects in single bounds: car roofs in traffic, the heads of a crowd, a row of utility poles, the backs of the wani. Used for travel and combat.
  - **Wani-watari:** teaches the MC to cross between realms by the wani (see 01).
  - **Thread-reading:** can see all threads and read the 49-day clocks.
  - **Combat:** fast, kick-based, acrobatic. Strong back legs. Fights about at Ōyōkai level and hides how strong they are; their actual power as a senior messenger is Kishin-level.
  - **Medicine:** the cattail pollen heals wounds and burns.
  - **Bureaucracy:** Inaba knows every rule of the Hare Office and the Kamiari Council and how to bend each one.
- **Fears:** the sea, sharks, the wani. They cross the wani regularly anyway.
- **Why Inaba is not on the ledger:**
  - The official explanation, which Inaba gives freely: **shinshi do not have red threads.** A messenger sets their thread aside with their god when they enter service.
  - The truth, which Inaba tells no one: Inaba never set their thread aside. **When the wani skinned them, their red thread went with the skin.** Ōkuninushi accepted them as a messenger anyway. The skin, and the thread, are worn as a cape by the **White-skin Wani** (Shirozame), a wani-lord of the Inaba Sea (see 13). Whoever Inaba's thread leads to is unknown.
  - If Inaba ever recovered their skin, they would have to choose between their thread and their service to Ōkuninushi. They have avoided thinking about this for several thousand years.
- **Relationship with the MC:** guide, teacher, friend, nag, accomplice. Not a romantic partner. Inaba helps the MC tie 108 knots and watches every one.

## 5. Other Gods

### Sukunabikona (少名毘古那)
- **Domain:** medicine, brewing, agriculture, hot springs. Ruler of **Tokoyo**.
- **Appearance:** tiny, about the height of a thumb, dressed in a moth-wing cloak, riding in a boat made from a seed pod. Loud for his size.
- **Relationship:** Ōkuninushi's old partner in building the land. He left for Tokoyo when the work was done.
- **Role:** at Ōkuninushi's request, he **leases a fragment of Tokoyo** to the MC as the Mayoiga's garden (see 11). Rent: one cask of sake brewed in the Mayoiga, delivered to Izumo each Kamiarizuki. He takes a lively interest in the MC's children and visits unannounced.

### Funado (岐神): God of Crossroads and Boundaries
- **Origin:** in the myth, when Izanagi fled Yomi, he threw down his staff at the boundary, and it became **Funado-no-kami**, the god who says "no further," guarding the line between the living and the dead. Later worshipped as the god of crossroads, village boundaries and travelers, in thousands of small roadside shrines (**dōsojin**).
- **Status:** shrineless. His roadside shrines were cleared away by road-widening and consolidation. He is a Yadonashi-gami.
- **Appearance:** an old man, short and broad, with a weathered face, a bald head, a white beard, a walking staff of old wood, and a construction worker's jacket over a faded kimono.
- **Role:** **manager of the Kamidomari** in Sanya (see 03). Unofficial elder of Tokyo's shrineless gods.
- **Knowledge:** because he was made to guard the boundary of Yomi, **he feels the Chibiki-iwa**. He has felt it cracking since 2005. He knows the Wager is being lost and that the Magatsu are a symptom. He is the person who first explains Yomi's rise to the MC.
- **Personality:** gruff, laconic, kind underneath, a heavy drinker of cheap shōchū. Old-fashioned.
- **Not a ledger partner.**

### Tachikiri-no-Ōkami (断切大神): Goddess of Severance
- **Domain:** cutting bonds. Every bond-cutting shrine in Japan (enkiri shrines, where people pray to be freed from abusive partners, addictions, illness and bad luck) holds a fraction of her.
- **Legitimacy:** her work is necessary. Bad bonds must be cut. Victims of abuse, addicts and the grieving pray to her and are freed.
- **Appearance:** a tall woman with short black hair cut perfectly straight, in a black kimono with a pattern of scissors and falling threads. She carries a pair of long iron shears at her hip. Her eyes are grey.
- **Relationship to the Jingikan:** her priests trained the Hasami-gumi. She did not object to the Seal in 1914, because she believed cutting the realms apart would spare humans from yokai predation.
- **Relationship to the Kirigami-shū:** the cult worships her. She has not endorsed them, and has not stopped them.
- **Stance on the MC:** opposed, at first. She believes 108 bonds held by one mortal is an unnatural knot that will strangle the MC and everyone tied to them. She attends the Kamiari Council to argue against the MC.
- **Ledger:** **Tachikiri holds one of the 108 threads.** Her thread is orphaned because her destined partner on the other side was cut away by her own priests during the Seal. She does not know this. See 14.

### Orihime (織姫): The Weaver Star
- **Domain:** weaving, the star Vega, the Tanabata festival.
- **Story:** the celestial weaver princess who married the cowherd Hikoboshi and was separated from him by the Milky Way. They meet once a year, on the seventh night of the seventh month.
- **Relationship:** **Tsumugi's teacher.** Tsumugi spun thread for Orihime's loom for a thousand years before she came down to Hachiōji.
- **Role:** on Tanabata night, Orihime's power weakens the Shimenawa for one night. She is sympathetic to the MC. She lives in the sky and can be contacted only on Tanabata.

### Izanami (伊邪那美)
- **Domain:** creation, death. Queen of Yomi.
- **State:** asleep beyond the Chibiki-iwa. The cracking of the boulder is slowly waking her.
- See 13.

### Izanagi (伊邪那岐)
- **Domain:** creation. The father of the gods.
- **State:** withdrawn to the **Hi-no-Wakamiya** ("Palace of the Young Sun") on Awaji island. He has not spoken in a thousand years. Izanagi's side of the Wager (more births than deaths) is held up only by the living. He does not act.

### Watatsumi (綿津見) and Otohime (乙姫)
- **Watatsumi:** the sea god, sovereign of Ryūgū and of the seas of both worlds. Vast, remote, and only interested in the sea.
- **Otohime:** his daughter, princess of the Dragon Palace, who in the folktale hosted Urashima Tarō and gave him the box that aged him three hundred years. She manages Ryūgū day to day. **A ledger partner** (see 14).

### Inari (稲荷), Uka-no-Mitama
- **Domain:** rice, prosperity, business.
- **State:** the goddess herself has been silent since the 1950s, present in her tens of thousands of shrines but no longer speaking. Her kitsune messengers run the Inari Group "in her name." Some foxes believe she withdrew in disapproval of the company's practices.

### Konohanasakuya-hime (木花之佐久夜毘売)
- **Domain:** Mount Fuji, blossoms, childbirth, volcanoes.
- **Relationship:** in myth, she gave birth in a burning hut to prove her children's legitimacy. She is the patron goddess of childbirth, and the MC's births interest her. She appears in public in Kakuriyo on the first day of each year.

### Amaterasu and the Heavenly Gods
- Distant. They do not act in this setting.

## 6. Komainu: A-gyō and Un-gyō

Komainu are the stone lion-dog guardians placed in pairs at shrine entrances. **A-gyō** (阿形) has an open mouth, sounding "a," the first sound, the beginning. **Un-gyō** (吽形) has a closed mouth, sounding "un," the last sound, the end. Together they are *a-un*: beginning and end, in harmony.

### Un-gyō ("Un")
- **Post:** left side of Itokake Shrine (Hachiōji). Stayed when the shrine was closed and has guarded the empty grounds for 117 years.
- **Forms:** a stone komainu statue, or a lion-dog person about 170 cm tall with a curly stone-grey mane, gold eyes, a sturdy build and grey stone-textured skin on the forearms and shins.
- **Pronouns:** they/them; presents androgynously.
- **Personality:** stoic, terse, deadpan. Says little; most often just "*Un.*" (which in Japanese is also a casual "yeah"). Fiercely loyal to Tsumugi. Slowly warms to the MC. A source of dry comedy.
- **Combat:** strong, very hard to injure, defensive. Can turn to stone at will.
- **Not a ledger partner.**
- **Wants:** A-gyō back.

### A-gyō ("A")
- **Post:** taken with the Itomaki in 1909. Now stands in the **Itomaki Vault** at Sōmusubi Jingū, bound by a vault seal that forces obedience to the Jingi-haku.
- **Forms:** a stone komainu, or a lion-dog person, taller and broader than Un, with an open, fanged mouth and a loud voice.
- **Pronouns:** they/them.
- **Personality:** talkative, angry, ashamed. Has spent 117 years guarding the thing that killed their shrine.
- **Role:** the final guardian of the Itomaki. When the MC reaches the vault, A-gyō must fight them unless the vault seal is broken.
- **Not a ledger partner.**
