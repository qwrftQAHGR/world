# 07. Peoples and Powers

## 1. Kemonobito (獣人): Beastfolk

### What They Are
Kemonobito are animal-descended people who developed human intelligence and speech over thousands of years in Kakuriyo's dense reiatsu. They make up **68%** of Kakuriyo's population. Spiritually they are ordinary (rank **Kotsu**): mortal lifespans of about 80–100 years, keen animal senses and small innate abilities, but no real powers.

Many yokai species have a kemonobito "cousin" species: fox-folk and kitsune, cat-folk and nekomata, raccoon-dog-folk and bake-danuki, crow-folk and karasu-tengu. Before 1914, kemonobito who lived long or received enough faith could **ascend** into their yokai cousin species. Since the Seal, ascension has almost stopped. The MC's Knot makes it reliable again.

### Appearance: The Three Builds
Each kemonobito is born with one of three builds. Build runs in families but varies within them.

| Build | Name | Description | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mimi-gata** (耳型, "ear type") | Mostly human | Human face and body, with animal ears, tail, and sometimes eyes or fangs | 45% |
| **Han-gata** (半型, "half type") | Mixed | Fur over much of the body, partial muzzle, animal hands or feet, digitigrade legs in some species | 30% |
| **Zen-gata** (全型, "full type") | Full anthro | Fully furred or feathered or scaled, animal head, upright humanoid body | 25% |

### Social Position
Beastfolk are the backbone of Kakuriyo's workforce and the majority of its voters, but their votes count least under rank-weighted voting. They form most of the Kemono Labor Party's base. Promotion to yokai rank through the MC's Knot is the most dramatic change in social status possible in Kakuriyo.

## 2. Yokai (妖怪)

Yokai are spiritually potent beings with real innate powers and long lifespans (200–1,000 years, sometimes more). They make up 21% of Kakuriyo's population and dominate its politics, business and military. The major species follow.

### Kitsune (狐): Foxes
- **Appearance:** human form with fox ears and one to nine tails, depending on rank. True form: a large fox, or a fox-human hybrid. Fur colors from red to gold, white, silver and (rarely) black.
- **Profile:** 2/1/2/5. Masters of Kushimitama.
- **Powers:** foxfire (kitsunebi), blue-white flames they can throw, hold and use as light. Illusion. Henge (disguise). Possession (illegal). Charm.
- **Rank markers:** tails (see 02).
- **Weaknesses:** dogs see through their illusions. Mirrors reveal their true form. Strong surprise can make their henge slip. Aburaage is a well-known weakness of willpower.
- **Culture:** sophisticated, status-conscious, business-minded. Kitsune were traditionally the messengers of Inari, the god of rice and prosperity, and they run the Inari Group "in Inari's name." Gender is fluid for many; femboy and NB kitsune are common.
- **Institution:** the **Inari Group**, governed by the **Myōbu Council** of twelve senior foxes. Chair: **Kuzunoha Akane**, seven tails, Kishin (see 14).
- **Stance on the MC:** wants an exclusive arrangement: the MC's Grace for Inari's people, the MC's children registered under Inari, the MC's household on Inari's payroll.

### Tengu (天狗): Mountain Goblins
- **Two kinds:** **karasu-tengu** (crow tengu), with crow features, black wings and beaks or partly human faces; and **daitengu** (great tengu), with red faces, long noses and great wings. Karasu-tengu become daitengu as they rise in rank.
- **Profile:** 5/2/1/2. Warriors.
- **Powers:** flight, wind (feather fans that create gales), swordsmanship, the **shakujō** (ringed staff), mountain ascetic arts shared with the yamabushi, and teleportation between mountains (Kishin only).
- **Weaknesses:** pride. In folklore, tengu are often humbled by being tricked. Their power weakens if their prayer beads are cut.
- **Culture:** disciplined, hierarchical, honor-bound, traditionalist. They ran Kakuriyo for three centuries (the Kurama Shogunate) and still run its military, police and border.
- **Institution:** **Kurama Command** (military), **Kebiishi-chō** (police), **Sakai-bugyō** (border). Supreme commander: **Sōjōbō**, the legendary king of the Kurama tengu who taught Yoshitsune swordsmanship, about 1,200 years old, Kishin, rarely seen.
- **Notable:** **Captain Karasuma Hayate**, karasu-tengu, Hyakki police captain (see 14).
- **Stance on the MC:** the MC must be registered, licensed and placed under military protection. Privately, the old tengu want the MC to restore human worship and the yamabushi tradition.

### Kappa (河童): River Imps
- **Appearance:** green or blue-green skin, webbed hands and feet, a turtle shell on the back, a beak-like mouth, and a **dish (sara)** on top of the head that holds water. Their human form keeps the dish under a hat or hair.
- **Profile:** 3/3/1/3.
- **Powers:** water manipulation, swimming, great strength while the dish is full, healing and bone-setting (in legend, kappa taught humans medicine), engineering intuition.
- **Weaknesses:** **if the water in the dish spills, they lose their strength.** Kappa are compulsively polite, and bowing to a kappa makes it bow back and spill its water. They are hopelessly fond of cucumbers.
- **Culture:** curious, inventive, obsessive, competitive. Love sumo. Kappa built most of Kakuriyo's modern technology.
- **Institution:** **Kawataro Industries**. Chair: **Kawataro Sōhei**, Ōyōkai, 380 years old, a cautious old engineer.
- **Notable:** **Kawanobe Kyū**, R&D engineer (see 14).
- **Stance on the MC:** scientific fascination. Wants to study the MC, the blessings and the Mayoiga.

### Tanuki (狸): Raccoon-Dogs
- **Appearance:** round, cheerful, with raccoon-dog ears and a thick striped tail. True form: a large tanuki. Famous for their round bellies, which they drum.
- **Profile:** 2/2/3/3.
- **Powers:** shapeshifting (better than kitsune at turning into objects and places), illusion, belly-drumming (a sound that confuses and charms), luck.
- **Weaknesses:** greed, sake, overconfidence. Their transformations break if they laugh too hard.
- **Culture:** merry, commercial, theatrical, sly. Kakuriyo's entertainers, merchants and smugglers.
- **Institution:** the **Bunbuku Group**, ruled by the **Hachihyakuya** ("Eight Hundred and Eight Houses"), the 808 tanuki families of Shikoku, under their boss **Inugami Gyōbu**, a Kishin tanuki over 900 years old who lives in Matsuyama.
- **Notable:** **Shigaraki Mame**, smuggler and idol manager (see 14).
- **Stance on the MC:** the MC is the biggest story in a century. The tanuki want broadcast rights, merchandise and a reality show.

### Oni (鬼): Ogres
- **Appearance:** large (2–3 meters), with red, blue, black or yellow skin, one to three horns, fangs, and enormous strength. They traditionally wear tiger-skin loincloths; modern oni wear work clothes.
- **Profile:** 6/1/2/1.
- **Powers:** overwhelming strength and endurance, iron clubs (kanabō), resistance to injury, and the ability to drink almost anything.
- **Weaknesses:** **roasted soybeans** (thrown on Setsubun, the bean-throwing festival, they burn oni like hot coals). **Holly and sardine heads** at doorways keep them out. Hot-tempered and easily provoked.
- **Culture:** working-class, loyal, loud, generous, union-proud. Oni built Kakuriyo's cities and ships.
- **Institution:** the **Ōe Federation of Labor** (Ōe Rōren), seated in Ōeyama and Ōe-gashi. Federation chair: **Ibaraki Kasane** (see 14). The **Shuten-kai**, a criminal syndicate named after the old oni king Shuten-dōji, runs protection and gambling in Ōe-gashi. Its boss is **Kumasaka**, a black-skinned oni.
- **Stance on the MC:** the MC's blessings for workers and beastfolk would change the balance of power. The Federation wants them. Every year on Setsubun, the oni unions strike in protest at bean-throwing.

### Jorōgumo (絡新婦): Spider Weavers
- **Appearance:** human form: elegant, often dressed in fine kimono, with dark eyes and long hands. Partial form: spider legs from the back (two to eight, by rank). True form: a human upper body on a giant spider lower body.
- **Profile:** 2/2/1/5.
- **Powers:** silk of any strength and fineness, weaving, webs, entrapment, poison, charm, and thread-craft, including (illegally) **forging red threads**.
- **Weaknesses:** fire burns their silk. In legend, a jorōgumo's thread can be cut by reciting the name of the waterfall god (the legend of Jōren Falls).
- **Culture:** meticulous, patient, traditional, artistic. Kakuriyo's weavers, tailors and network engineers.
- **Institution:** **Jōren Weaveworks**, headquartered at Jōren Falls. CEO: **Jōren no Ōkumo**, an 800-year-old Kishin jorōgumo, reclusive. The **Itokake-za** weavers' guild in Hyakki is affiliated but independent.
- **Notable:** **Ayame of Itokake-chō** (see 14). **Ōgumo Shizuri**, a thread-forger (see 13).
- **Stance on the MC:** Itokake-chō is devoted to the MC as Tsumugi's chosen. Jōren corporate fears the MC's household will expose the forgers' black market, and fears Enjō reaching Kumo-net if the boundary opens.

### Nekomata (猫又): Forked-Tail Cats
- **Appearance:** cat ears, a tail that forks into two by rank, slit-pupiled eyes. True form: a large cat with a forked tail and ghost-flames at the tips.
- **Profile:** 3/1/3/3.
- **Powers:** agility, ghost-fire (onibi), animating the dead (an old, illegal art), seeing and hunting Mushi and Magatsu, luck.
- **Weaknesses:** catnip-equivalents (matatabi, silvervine). Water. Boredom.
- **Culture:** independent, playful, vain, nocturnal. Many are streamers, entertainers and freelance exterminators. No central institution; organized in loose clans.
- **Ancestral home:** Mount Nekomata, Ecchū.
- **Notable:** **Tamaki ("TamaTama")**, streamer (see 14).
- **Stance on the MC:** fascination and gossip. Several nekomata clans want contracts as the MC's exterminators.

### Yuki-onna (雪女): Snow Women
- **Appearance:** pale, white or ice-blue hair, cold skin, white kimono. Mostly female-presenting; male yuki-otoko exist and are rarer.
- **Profile:** 3/2/0/5.
- **Powers:** ice, snow, cold, freezing breath, blizzards, the "kiss of frost" (draining warmth).
- **Weaknesses:** heat, fire, summer. In legend, a yuki-onna who falls in love and is betrayed melts.
- **Culture:** reserved, proud, northern, isolationist.
- **Institution:** the **Snow Coalition** (Yukiguni Rengō). Head: **Shirakami Fuyuhime**, Kishin, 700 years old.
- **Notable:** **Yukimura Sayo**, envoy (see 14).
- **Stance on the MC:** opposed to reopening, but internally divided. Yuki-onna birth rates are among the worst in Kakuriyo.

### Baku (獏): Dream-Eaters
- **Appearance:** tapir-like or elephant-like features, a short trunk, small tusks, often patterned like a tapir. Human form with trunk-like noses and soft grey skin.
- **Profile:** 1/5/1/3.
- **Powers:** eating nightmares, entering dreams, eating Mushi and small Magatsu, sleep medicine.
- **Culture:** gentle, clinical, neutral.
- **Institution:** the **Baku Medical Network**, Yume-no-shima. Director: **Dr. Kiyose Mugen**.
- **Notable:** **Dr. Yumemi** (see 14).
- **Stance on the MC:** concerned with health and with Yomi. The baku have been seeing Yomi in the nightmares of patients since 2005.

### Tsukumogami (付喪神): Awakened Objects
- **Origin:** an object that reaches **one hundred years old** awakens a spirit. Umbrellas (kasa-obake), lanterns (chōchin-obake), sandals, teapots, sewing machines, phones.
- **Appearance:** varies. Low-rank tsukumogami are their object with eyes and limbs. Higher ranks gain a human form with features of the object.
- **Bound to the object:** if the original object is destroyed, the tsukumogami dies.
- **Modern note:** objects from the 1920s are now becoming tsukumogami. A wave of radios, gramophones and Taishō-era clothing is awakening.
- **Notable:** **Shinga**, a 1905 Singer sewing machine (see 14).

## 3. Ijin (異人): Foreign Monsters

### Origin
Monsters from the hidden realms of other lands. The first came as traders through Ura-Dejima after 1641. The great waves came in the 1700s and 1800s as Europe's hidden realms faded during the Western Dimming. Others came from Hōrai (the Chinese hidden realm), from the Ottoman and Persian hiddens, and from elsewhere.

### The Problem of Fuel
Ijin have **no roots in Japanese belief**. Japanese humans did not historically fear or worship them, so they cannot draw on Japanese Osore or Shinkō. They survive on their innate reserves, on the faith of their own communities' chapels and shrines, and on what little foreign belief reaches them. Most ijin are permanently **low on fuel** and weaker than they were in their homelands.

**The MC's Knot naturalizes an ijin's soul** (see 10): it ties them to the land so that they can draw on Japanese Shinkō. This would transform the ijin community, which is why Ura-Dejima's leaders want it.

### Ura-Dejima and Shin-Dejima
- **Ura-Dejima** (Hizen province): the original foreign port. Now a city of 600,000 built on and around the old fan-shaped trading island, with European-style stone buildings, Hōrai pagodas, onion domes and harbors full of ships from the hidden seas.
- **Shin-Dejima** (Hyakki): the capital's foreign quarter (see 06).
- **Government:** the **Dejima Council**, led by the **Kapitan**, a title taken from the head of the Dutch trading post. Current Kapitan: **Mireille Aubade**, a 600-year-old lamia matriarch from a Mediterranean hidden realm, who has held the post for 140 years.
- **Political status:** ijin have observer seats in the Yagyō-in but no votes.

## 4. Kakushi (隠し): Kakuriyo's Humans

- **Origin:** descendants of humans **spirited away** (kamikakushi) from Utsushiyo over the centuries: children who wandered into forests at twilight, travelers who took a wrong turn on a mountain path, people who simply vanished. Before 1914 thousands a year crossed this way. Now several hundred a year still do.
- **Population:** about 1 million, concentrated in Ueno-Kakushi in Hyakki and in old mountain villages.
- **Traits:** ordinary humans, rank Kotsu, but with one valuable trait: like all humans, **they generate Osore and Shinkō** at human levels. Kakushi prayer is worth far more than yokai prayer. Kakushi priests are prized by shrines.
- **Some Kakushi have sight** at higher rates than Utsushiyo humans (about 1 in 20) from generations of living in dense reiatsu.
- **Role:** brokers, translators, experts on Utsushiyo things, shrine priests.
- **Institution:** the **House of Kakushi**, which holds one seat in the Ie-no-Ma. Head: **Kamikakushi no Genzō**, 81, a descendant of a boy spirited away in 1781.
- **Notable:** **Mayu**, broker (see 14).
- **Stance on the MC:** the MC is the first human in a century who can go home and come back. Every Kakushi family has a lost ancestor whose relatives in Utsushiyo they would like to find.

## 5. Shinshi (神使): Divine Messenger Lineages

Certain animal lineages serve particular gods as messengers. Members of these lineages (kemonobito or yokai) can be raised directly to the rank of **shinshi** by their god, taking the god's service as their life's work. Shinshi have no red threads of their own; their bond is to their god (silver thread).

| Lineage | God | Shrine |
|---|---|---|
| White hares | Ōkuninushi | Izumo (the Hare Office) |
| Foxes | Inari | Fushimi |
| Three-legged crows (Yatagarasu) | Kumano gods | Kumano |
| Deer | Kasuga gods | Kasuga (Nara) |
| Doves | Hachiman | Iwashimizu |
| Monkeys | Hie gods | Hie |
| Wolves | Mitsumine gods | Mitsumine |
| Snakes | Benzaiten | Enoshima, Chikubushima |
| Rats | Daikokuten | various |

Shinshi rank is prestigious. A kemonobito of a messenger lineage who receives the MC's Knot can be promoted to shinshi-equivalent status without losing their thread. This has never happened before.
