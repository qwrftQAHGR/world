# 10. Blessings: The Kago System (加護)

## 1. Overview

Every sincere sexual union with the MC grants a **blessing (kago)**. The first union with a destined partner grants a very large blessing. Later unions grant progressively smaller ones, levelling off at a small permanent floor that also purifies. Births renew the large blessing.

Blessings are measured in **K**. **1 K** is the size of one full Knot blessing, enough to raise a beastfolk to full yokai.

---

## 2. Blessing Values

| Blessing | When | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Knot** (Musubime, 結び目) | First sincere union with a **ledger partner** | **1 K**, plus type-specific effects (section 4) | Ties the thread. Grants a Mitamashiro form (see 12). |
| **Grace** (Megumi, 恵み) | First sincere union with **anyone not on the ledger** | **0.25 K** | No thread, no Mitamashiro, no sibling threads. |
| **Echo** (Kodama, 木霊) | Later unions, counted **once per day per partner** | Half of the previous step | From a Knot: 0.5, 0.25, 0.125, 0.0625. From a Grace: 0.125, 0.0625. |
| **Hearth** (Kamado, 竈) | Every sincere union once Echo has fallen below the floor | **0.02 K** (counted once per day per partner) **plus complete kegare cleansing** | The permanent floor. Never reaches zero. |
| **Seventh-Night** (Shichiya, 七夜) | Each **birth** of a child with the MC as a parent | **1 K** to the non-MC parent | Once per birth event (twins and litters count once). Also see section 6. |

### How Echo and Hearth Work in Practice
- A ledger partner's blessings over their first unions: Knot (1 K), then 0.5, 0.25, 0.125, 0.0625, then Hearth (0.02 K a day) from the sixth day of unions on.
- **Only the first union each day counts** toward Echo or Hearth value. Additional unions that day still give Hearth **cleansing**, but no further K.
- **Hearth cleansing is complete:** both the partner and the MC drop to 0 kegare, at every sincere union, as often as they happen.
- Over a year, a partner with the MC most days accumulates about **6–7 K from Hearth alone**, plus 1 K per child.

### Example: A Partner's First Year
A kitsune at Yōkai rank (2 tails) is knotted:
- Knot: 1 K
- Echo, first four days: 0.94 K
- Hearth, about 300 days: 6 K
- Two children: 2 K
- **Total: about 10 K.** Promotion to Ōyōkai costs 2 K and promotion to Kishin a further 4 K, so by the end of the year this kitsune is a **Kishin** (6–8 tails) with K to spare. Before the MC, this would have taken her five hundred years.

## 3. Sincerity (Makoto, 誠)

| Kind of union | Multiplier | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Sincere**: real desire and real feeling, freely given | ×1 | Full blessing |
| **Transactional**: for advantage, duty or payment, without real feeling | ×0.5 | Half blessing. Does **not** tie a ledger knot. |
| **Coerced**: by force, threat, blackmail, magic, drugs or deception about consent | ×0 | No blessing. A ledger thread snaps. The coercer is cursed (see 09). |

**Sincerity cannot be faked.** The blessing reads what is actually in the heart. A consort sent by a House who sincerely likes the MC receives full Grace. One who is only following orders receives half. A partner who is ambivalent but genuinely wants the MC in the moment is sincere.

## 4. What the Knot Does: By Partner Type

The Knot always grants **1 K** toward rank. It also grants a **type-specific effect** that only the Knot provides. Grace grants 0.25 K and **none** of these type-specific effects.

### Utsushiyo Human, No Yokai Heritage
- **1 K (Knot): Awakening.**
  - The partner gains **spiritual sight**.
  - They manifest a **signature ability** from their dominant mitama. Examples:
    - **Ara:** superhuman strength, speed or reflexes; striking with spiritual force; a weapon art.
    - **Nigi:** healing hands; barriers; calming others; making plants grow.
    - **Saki:** luck manipulation; sensing danger; finding lost things; blessing or cursing objects.
    - **Kushi:** illusions; insight into lies; prophetic dreams; speaking with animals or objects.
  - They become rank-equivalent to **Yōkai**, as an **Awakened human (Kakusei-bito, 覚醒人)**.
- **2 K total:** a second ability. Immunity to ordinary illness. Lifespan extended to about 150 years.
- **3 K total: Turning (Utsuri-gaeri).** The partner may choose to **become a yokai** of a species matching their soul profile (an Ara-dominant partner might become an oni or a tengu; a Kushi-dominant one a kitsune or a jorōgumo; and so on). They keep their mind, memories and personality and gain the species' body and powers. They may also refuse and remain an Awakened human, whose power keeps growing on the same K scale. **The choice belongs entirely to the partner.**

### Utsushiyo Human With Hidden Heritage
Many humans carry yokai ancestry from intermarriage before 1914 and from the Nokori afterward.
- **1 K (Knot): Senzogaeri (先祖返り, "ancestral return").** The ancestral blood wakes.
  - The partner gains spiritual sight, **an ancestral form**, and that species' basic powers at **Yōkai** rank.
  - **The partner's heart decides how deep the change goes:** anywhere from mimi-gata (ears and a tail) to full true form. They can switch between their human form and their ancestral form at will (utsuri).
  - Examples: a quarter-kitsune grows fox ears, a tail and foxfire. A descendant of a yuki-onna develops frost breath and white hair in her true form. A family with tengu blood produces black wings.
- **Further K** raises them through yokai ranks normally.

### Kemonobito (Beastfolk)
- **1 K (Knot): Ascension.** The partner is promoted from Kotsu to **Yōkai**.
  - A fox-folk becomes a kitsune with a first tail and foxfire.
  - A cat-folk becomes a nekomata with a split tail.
  - A raccoon-dog-folk becomes a bake-danuki with shapeshifting.
  - A crow-folk becomes a karasu-tengu with wings and wind.
  - Species with no common yokai cousin become yokai of their own animal kind, with appropriate powers: a boar-folk becomes a great boar spirit with earth-shaking charges; a deer-folk becomes a forest deer spirit with healing and the voice of the wood; a rabbit-folk becomes a moon rabbit with speed and luck.
  - **Messenger lineages** (deer, crows, wolves, hares, foxes, snakes) may choose instead to become **shinshi-equivalent**, with a silver thread to their lineage's god, *without* losing their red thread to the MC. This has never been possible before.
- **Social effect:** a beastfolk's vote weight doubles, and their legal rank, job prospects and lifespan change overnight. To Kakuriyo's beastfolk this is the most significant thing about the MC.

### Yokai
- **1 K (Knot): Rank progress**, plus **stabilization.** A fading yokai stops fading permanently. Their soul profile may be rebalanced by 1 point toward whatever the partner wishes (for example, an Ara-7 oni may reduce Ara to 6 and raise Nigi, ending the risk of rampage).
- **Promotion costs** (see 02): Yōkai → Ōyōkai, 2 K. Ōyōkai → Kishin, 4 K. Kishin → Kami, 8 K and a shrine.
- **Visible markers** change with rank: kitsune tails, tengu wings and noses, oni horns, nekomata tail forks, jorōgumo legs, kappa shells (see 02).

### Ijin (Foreign Monsters)
- **1 K (Knot): Naturalization of the soul.** The Knot ties the ijin to the land of Japan.
  - They can draw on **Japanese Shinkō and Osore** for the first time, which ends their permanent fuel shortage.
  - Their power in Japan rises to what it would be in their homeland.
  - Kakuriyo's rank system recognizes them fully.
- Plus 1 K of rank progress.
- **Political effect:** every naturalized ijin becomes a full member of Japanese spiritual society. Ura-Dejima's leaders consider this the most important development since 1641.

### Toshi-densetsu (Urban-Legend Yokai)
- **1 K (Knot): Freedom from the script.**
  - The partner is no longer compelled to act out their legend. Kuchisake-onna does not have to ask "Am I pretty?"
  - They no longer depend on being feared. They can live on the MC's En.
  - They can choose a new name and a new appearance within their nature.
- Plus 1 K of rank progress.

### Tsukumogami (Awakened Objects)
- **1 K (Knot): Independence from the object.**
  - The partner's life is no longer bound to their original object. If the object is destroyed, they survive.
  - They gain a full human form, if they did not already have one.
- Plus 1 K of rank progress.

### Kami (Gods), Including Shrineless Gods
- **1 K (Knot): Sustenance.**
  - The god gains the MC's En as a permanent source of sustenance. **Fading stops** forever, even without a shrine.
  - Kami do not rank up (they are already rank 1). Instead, K adds to their **power**. 1 K is roughly equal to a year of worship at a village shrine.
- **Shrine restoration:** a shrineless god knotted to the MC can have their old shrine site rebuilt. With the MC's En, a restored shrine fills with Shinkō much faster than normal.

## 5. How the Realm Shapes the Blessing

### In Utsushiyo: Folded Blessings (Tatami-kago, 畳み加護)
In Utsushiyo's thin atmosphere and under the Veil, blessings **fold**: their outward effects are compressed into forms that look plausible.

- **Outside the Ura** (in ordinary life), a folded blessing looks like luck, health and talent: a promotion, a sudden recovery from illness, a skill the person "always had," a streak of good fortune. **Physical transformations do not appear.**
- **Inside the Ura** (during a fight, inside a kekkai, or when the partner deliberately steps into the spiritual layer), the partner can use their powers at **full strength**, and physical forms appear (wings, tails, horns). They return to normal when the partner leaves the Ura.
- **Spiritual sight** is never folded. It works at all times.

### In Kakuriyo: Unfolding
- The first time a partner spends **a full night (sunset to sunrise) in Kakuriyo** after being blessed, all folded blessings **unfold at once**. Physical transformations appear in full; powers are fully available.
- Unfolding is dramatic and sometimes painful: tails grow in over minutes, wings unfold, horns push through. It often happens in public, and in Hyakki it usually ends up on Kumo-net.
- **After unfolding,** the partner can switch freely between human form and true form in **either realm** (utsuri). In Utsushiyo, their human form remains the comfortable default.

### Blessings Received in Kakuriyo
Blessings received in Kakuriyo **manifest immediately and visibly**: tails appear, ranks jump, in front of witnesses.

## 6. Seventh-Night Blessings and Births

- **When:** at each birth of a child with the MC as one parent. Births happen on the **seventh night** of gestation (see 11).
- **To the non-MC parent:** **1 K**, at the moment of birth.
- **To the MC:** no K. Instead: a large surge of En, the next stage of breast growth (see 11) and the start or increase of lactation.
- **To the child:** every child of the MC is born **at Yōkai rank** (or the equivalent for their species), regardless of the other parent's rank, carrying the birth blessing. This applies to children of human and beastfolk partners as well.
- **Multiples:** twins, triplets and litters count as one birth event and give 1 K, not more.

**Effect:** births reset the diminishing returns. A partner whose blessings have fallen to the Hearth floor gets a full 1 K again with each child. The household has a strong incentive to have children, and Kakuriyo's Houses notice immediately.

| Mark | Who | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Musubi-ito no shirushi** (Knot mark) | Knotted partners | A thin red line around the base of the left little finger, like a tied thread. Permanent. |

These marks are visible to anyone in Kakuriyo and to those with sight in Utsushiyo. **Counterfeit Grace marks** (tattoos and illusions) appear on Hyakki's black market within weeks of the MC becoming known.

## 8. What the MC Receives

The MC does not gain K or rank from blessings. The MC's power grows by **number of knots** (see 12). From each Knot, the MC gains:

1. **En:** the MC's fuel pool grows permanently.
2. **A Mitamashiro form:** the partner can become a weapon in the MC's hands (see 12).
3. **An Echo trait (Kodama-ke, 木霊気):** a small, permanent trait drawn from the partner's nature. Examples:

| Partner | Echo trait |
|---|---|
| Kitsune | A tiny foxfire flame at the fingertip; can light candles and see in the dark |
| Tengu | Perfect balance; can't fall from a height without landing on their feet |
| Kappa | Can hold their breath for ten minutes; minor wounds close fast |
| Tanuki | Can make one small object look like another for a few seconds |
| Oni | Can drink any amount of alcohol without getting drunk; slightly stronger grip |
| Jorōgumo | Can climb walls by fingertips |
| Nekomata | Lands silently; can see Mushi without thread-sight |
| Yuki-onna | Immune to cold; can chill a drink by touch |
| Baku | Never has nightmares |
| Human (Awakened) | A weaker version of the partner's signature ability |
| Kami | A faint aura of the god's domain (a smell of rice for a harvest god, a sound of water for a well god) |

Echo traits are minor individually. With 108 of them, the MC accumulates a very wide toolkit.

From a **Grace**, the MC receives a small amount of En and nothing else.

## 9. Knock-On Effects

- **The Grace market.** Once the MC is known in Kakuriyo, Houses, corporations and individuals compete for Grace. The Inari Group proposes an exclusive contract. Noble Houses send sincere-seeming consorts. The Kemono Labor Party demands that Grace be distributed fairly among beastfolk. Black markets sell counterfeit Grace marks, "Musubi charms" and fake relics.
- **Forged partners.** Factions hire jorōgumo forgers to plant agents with counterfeit red threads in the MC's path (see 09, 13).
- **Political rank shifts.** Each beastfolk ascension doubles a vote. Each new Ōyōkai or Kishin shifts weight in the Machi-no-Ma. Over a year, the MC's household could change Kakuriyo's electoral math.
- **The House question.** Partners promoted by the Knot and children born at Yōkai rank count toward a House's requirements: **100 members of Yōkai rank or higher and one Kishin**. The MC's household can qualify for the vacant 100th seat in the Ie-no-Ma within its first year (see 07, 15).
- **Jealousy and politics inside the household.** Partners compare tails, wings and ranks. Some resent others' children. Sibling threads make this workable, not absent.
- **Religion in Utsushiyo.** As the Veil thins, rumors spread of a person at a Hachiōji shrine whose touch heals and brings luck. Pilgrims arrive. Some found a cult.
